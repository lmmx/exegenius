# 2026-10-14: the E8 sentence plateaus, and a second sentence takes Sonnet `high` to the ceiling (E8b, E9)

**The finding:**
- **The E8 sentence alone** lifts Sonnet `high` above E7 on most extractions, but not where E7 was already strong. It misses its preregistered bar: 3 of 5 new extractions, where 4 were needed.
- **The miss is specific:** the core result `label-residual-budget`.
