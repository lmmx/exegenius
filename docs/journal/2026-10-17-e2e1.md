Both scorings give the same verdict: the verdicts as first written, and as completed under the 22:58 completeness rule.
- The wiki is `docs/` on branch `171026-e2e-final-config` of `lmmx/openai-nlogn-wiki`, at commit 42727e0. It has 39 pages in 8 parts, built by exegenius 0.6.0 from `paper.tex` with production defaults: 3 audit rounds and 2 reader rounds. All 433 tasks were accepted.
- **The central chain is carried end to end.** Three blind Opus judges walked the built wiki with `chain-walk-v1`. Each found all nine central steps reconstructed, from the fixed network to the exponent $\tau=1-2^{-50}$, with the reason given and no breaks. The best earlier wiki reached 6 of 9 with 5 breaks (`docs/journal/2026-10-17-e2e1/chain-walk/`).
- Both plans reached the calibrated level: central 9/9, median faithful 19/19 and no major error (`plan-judge/`, `plan-judge-final/`).
- **One page misstates an algorithm.** packed-bit-addition drops the final XOR at $j_{f-1}$, which the paper performs at lines 2187–2192, so its procedure is incomplete and does nothing when $f=1$. Two of three judges call this major, and I checked it against the page and the paper. It was present from the first draft. Three audits missed it, and no reader opened the page.
- **The central page states more than it derives.** subspace-labels has median faithful 22 of 31 assigned steps, against the standard's 28, with no errors. All three judges find the binary orthonormal-basis lemma (S1.3.6.13–14) absent. Two find the per-edge residual table (S1.3.6.2–8) absent: the page shows the label chains as inclusions without computing the residual dimensions. The other four pages the chain walk cites meet the standard.
- 32 of 39 pages meet the full page standard, 16 of the 23 with steps (`page-judge-final/results.json`). The other 38 pages have no major error by median.
- **Simulated readers: HELPED, on the R2 arm.** This is observed with the validated page judge. Deficient pages carry 7.65 stuck points per page-round against 2.52 on sound ones; per visit the rates are 1.65 and 0.71. On subspace-labels, readers in both rounds named the judges' gap in their own words: the label table is "given as facts with no derivation". The repairs resolved little: 6 of 23 rewritten pages gained faithful steps, 1 lost one, one major error was removed, and subspace-labels stayed at 22/31. The readers' coverage scores remain unvalidated, at 18/18 on the chain goals, and held-out coverage did not change (20/24 in both rounds).
- Cost was 582.8 M cache read against an estimate of 260–390 M. The run's own tasks came to 154.5 M, evaluation to 136.1 M and orchestration to 292.2 M (`calibration/tools/spend.py`, now with E2E1's rules).
- `calibration/tools/e2e1pages.py` preps and scores page judging, reporting omitted ids. `calibration/tools/e2e1readers.py` computes R2 and R3 against the page judge.

## Missing

- The source-aware audit does not reliably catch a procedure with its last step missing: three audits passed packed-bit-addition. A2's planted errors did not test this kind of recall.
- Simulated readers reach only the pages on their goals' paths. Nothing makes them read a page no goal routes through, so they cannot find errors there.
- Reader-driven repairs do not reliably close the gaps the readers name: subspace-labels was rewritten on Opus `xhigh` after round 1 and did not move. No repair follows the last reader round, so round-2 stuck points are reported, not acted on.
- Writing hit the per-page derivation limit again on long key-step pages: simultaneous-layer 21/35 and subspace-labels 22/31. This is the limit W3 found in first drafts, and audits and repairs did not remove it.
- Judges omit dash-level scored ids in long assignments: 16 of 186 verdicts in this run. The completeness rule handles this, but the judge prompt or `e2e1pages.py` could prevent it.
- The result covers one run on one paper. It has no replicate, and the second document is issue #19.
- Orchestration from this session costs more than the run itself. No measurement yet compares it with a lighter driver.
