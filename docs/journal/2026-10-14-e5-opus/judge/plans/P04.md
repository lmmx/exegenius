  - method: Reduce the product to a convolution over a polynomial ring computed by Fourier-type transforms; supply the two fast tape procedures that remove the logarithmic factor from address movement and from butterfly arithmetic, both obtained from linear networks that save a power; then assemble the algorithm and tally every cost.
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning the data once per transform level already costs $n\log n$, so the logarithmic factor can only be reduced by savings in both data movement and arithmetic.
