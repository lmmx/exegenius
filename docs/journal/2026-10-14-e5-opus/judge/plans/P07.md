- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): One deterministic fixed-alphabet multitape Turing machine computes the exact $2n$-bit product of two $n$-bit integers in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.
  - method: build two tape procedures that beat a scan per level (fast chunk interchange and simultaneous butterfly layers) from finite linear networks with a rank deficit, place them in the Gaussian-resampling and synthetic-transform reduction of multiplication to convolution, recover the product exactly, and total the costs
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning $\Theta(n)$ bits at each of $\Theta(\log n)$ transform levels already costs $\Theta(n\log n)$, so both data movement and butterfly arithmetic must be done in less than a scan per level.
