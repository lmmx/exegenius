# Plan P01

The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): One deterministic fixed-alphabet multitape Turing machine computes the exact $2n$-bit product of two $n$-bit integers in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, disproving the $n\log n$ optimality conjecture in that model.
  - method: build two fast tape procedures (address interchange and simultaneous butterfly layers) from finite linear networks with a rank deficit, assemble them into the transform-based convolution algorithm, prove exact recovery and bound the total time
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning $\Theta(n)$ bits at each of $\Theta(\log n)$ transform levels already costs $\Theta(n\log n)$, so beating $n\log n$ requires savings in both data movement and arithmetic.
  - relies on: time-bound, exact-recovery, nlogn-conjecture, turing-machines

    - method: build the scalar exchange from three-subset invocations, attach nested subspace labels to its gates, and count the residual dimensions on the edges
    - motivation (explicit): Turning each edge's change of frame into smaller calls of the same procedure only pays off if the total number of such calls is less than wires times label dimension; keeping the dimension lost on decreasing edges small achieves that.
    - relies on: linear-network, frames, subspace-labels, bank-exchange, binary-field-xor, gaussian-dyadics
