# Plan P10

The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): A single deterministic multitape Turing machine with a fixed finite alphabet multiplies two $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, disproving the $n\log n$ optimality conjecture.
  - method: Reduce the product to a convolution of short digits, supply the convolution by power-of-two synthetic transforms whose tape layouts and butterfly layers are made cheap by two rank-saving tape procedures, recover the integers exactly by rounding and carries, and total the cost.
  - motivation (explicit): We therefore need savings in both movement and arithmetic.
  - relies on: integer-multiplication-conjecture, multitape-tm, big-o, logarithms-and-iterated-logarithm-notation
  - **S0.1** (show, page `multiplication-algorithm`): The pipeline of radix digits, the Chinese-remainder address map, two source transforms, a pointwise product, the opposite transform, known rescalings, rounding and carries returns exactly the $2n$-bit product of the two inputs.
    - method: Assemble the engine from the inside out: first the ring convolution, then Gaussian resampling and chirps that turn prime-axis transforms into it, then the layout, parameters and twist that fit the sizes together, then run the six steps and bound the rounding error.
    - motivation (explicit): Splitting the inputs into radix digits reduces multiplication to convolution of short integer coefficients.
    - relies on: radix-digit-convolution, crt-address-map, gaussian-resampling, permutation-cancellation, bluestein-chirp, phase-twist, ring-convolution, exact-recovery, parameter-choice, fixed-point-truncation, cyclic-convolution

      - method: Compute two forward synthetic transforms with the same retained frequency order, multiply records pointwise by signed packing, and apply the opposite transform, with every layer a simultaneous butterfly layer and every axis alignment a fast chunk interchange.
      - motivation (explicit): Entries of the transform are short polynomials in a quotient where the required roots of unity act by exact signed permutations of coefficient lists, so every transform step is a butterfly layer or a signed shift.
