# Plan P01

The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): A deterministic fixed multitape Turing machine multiplies two $n$-bit integers in time $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.
  - method: reduce multiplication to a convolution over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ via CRT, Gaussian resampling, chirps and a twist, and evaluate its transforms with fast interchange and butterfly layers
  - motivation (recoverable): Splitting into radix digits reduces multiplication to convolution; the transforms need power-of-two axes and polynomial roots of unity, and the two tape procedures supply the saving. The text builds the tape tools first and then the reductions.
  - relies on: multitape-model, nlogn-conjecture, radix-digit-convolution, convolution-dft, multidimensional-transform, big-o, polynomials
  - **S1** (show): Two tape procedures, fast address interchange and fast simultaneous butterfly layers, make address movement and Fourier layers cost less than a full scan per level.

    - motivation (explicit): Scanning an array of $\Theta(n)$ bits at each of $\Theta(\log n)$ transform levels already costs $\Theta(n\log n)$, so savings are needed in both movement and arithmetic.
    - relies on: fft-butterfly
