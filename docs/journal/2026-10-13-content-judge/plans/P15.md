# Plan P15

The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): Integer multiplication of $n$-bit integers takes $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time on one deterministic fixed-tape machine, disproving the $n\log n$ conjecture.
  - method: build the fast tape primitives (address interchange and simultaneous butterfly layers), then the synthetic transform and convolution machinery, then the resampling reductions and the assembly with its cost table
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning $\Theta(n)$ bits at each of $\Theta(\log n)$ transform levels costs $n\log n$, so savings are needed in both movement and arithmetic, and each primitive must exist before the transforms that use it.
  - relies on: nlogn-conjecture, convolution, big-o, multitape-tm, logarithms-and-exponentials, karatsuba-and-schoolbook-multiplication
  - **T1** (infer, page `array-layout`): An array on a product of ranges is stored in lexicographic order, so address fields, rows and the bit volume $V$ are positions in the tape stream.

  - **T2** (infer, page `stream-ops`): Splitting an array into streams by a digit or by cyclic rows, applying a pointwise map, and merging cost $O(V)$ tape steps.
