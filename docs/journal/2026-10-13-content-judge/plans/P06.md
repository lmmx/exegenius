The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `target`): A deterministic fixed-tape machine multiplies $n$-bit integers in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time, $\kappa=2^{-182}$.
  - method: reduce multiplication through a chain of representation changes down to ring convolution, using the fast tape procedures, then recover the product
  - motivation (recoverable): Each reduction changes the problem to one whose transforms have power-of-two axes and polynomial roots of unity, where the fast tape procedures apply.
  - relies on: nlogn-conjecture, big-o, lower-and-upper-bound-asymptotics, constants-and-parameters-in-complexity-bounds
