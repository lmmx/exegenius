  - method: Reduce multiplication to a convolution computed by synthetic polynomial transforms after Gaussian resampling and chirps, and speed up their address moves and butterfly layers with two network-based tape procedures
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning the array at each of the $\Theta(\log n)$ transform levels already costs $\Theta(n\log n)$, so both data movement and arithmetic must be sped up.
  - relies on: fixed-tape-model, nlogn-conjecture, hvdh-multiplier, big-o-notation

    - method: Build the networks from the three-subset gadget, attach frames and nested subspace labels to their gates, and sum the dimension changes along the edges
