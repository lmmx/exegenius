# Plan P05

The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): A deterministic fixed-tape Turing machine multiplies $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.
  - method: build two tape procedures that save a power of the width, use them inside the Harvey and van der Hoeven Gaussian-resampling multiplication with polynomial transforms and retained permutations, then choose parameters, prove exactness and sum the costs
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning the data at each of $\log n$ transform levels already costs $n\log n$, so beating it needs savings in both movement and arithmetic.
  - relies on: fixed-tape-model, nlogn-conjecture, hvdh-multiplier, big-o
  - **A** (show): Two tape procedures, an address-chunk interchange and a simultaneous butterfly layer, each cost a power $u^\tau$ or $d^{\lambda'}$ below the naive factor.
    - method: derive both procedures from fixed linear networks whose rank deficit $s<Wm$ gives a recursion that saves a power
    - motivation (explicit): A strict inequality between the number of smaller calls per level and the growth of the width is where the power saving comes from.
    - relies on: fixed-tape-model, tape-basics
