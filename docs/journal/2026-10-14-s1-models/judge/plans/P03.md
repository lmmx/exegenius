# Plan P03

The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): One deterministic fixed-alphabet, fixed-tape Turing machine computes the exact $2n$-bit product of two $n$-bit integers in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, disproving the $n\log n$ conjecture in this model.
  - method: Reduce the product to radix-digit convolution, move it by a Chinese-remainder map, Gaussian resampling, chirps and a phase twist to convolution over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ computed by synthetic transforms; speed up the transforms' address movement and butterfly layers with two network-based tape procedures; then prove exact recovery and total the costs.
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning $\Theta(n)$ stored bits at each of $\Theta(\log n)$ transform levels already costs $\Theta(n\log n)$, so beating $n\log n$ needs savings in both data movement and arithmetic.
  - relies on: nlogn-conjecture, hvdh-multiplier, radix-reduction, multitape-tm, big-o, worst-case-running-time
  - **A** (show): Two tape procedures save over plain scanning: address chunks of width $u$ are interchanged in $O(Vu^\tau)$ time, and a simultaneous normalized butterfly layer on $D\le d$ chunks costs $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$, with fixed $\tau,\lambda'<1$.
    - method: Build each procedure from its own fixed linear network that exchanges two banks and restores scratch wires, apply the network pointwise to row-split role streams with frames chosen so every edge is a few smaller calls, and solve the volume-normalized recurrence.
    - motivation (explicit): Because the smaller calls number $s<Wm$ and each acts on volume $V/W$, multiplying the parameter by $m$ raises normalized cost by only $s/W<m$; this strict inequality is the source of the power saving.
    - relies on: linear-network, row-splitting, frames, power-saving-recursion
    - **S1** (show, page `rank-deficit`): Two fixed finite networks (a bit network over $\mathbb F_2$ and a complex network over $\mathbb Z[i,1/2]$) exchange two banks while restoring scratch wires, and their label budgets lose $L<N/2$, so the total number $s$ of smaller calls their edges need satisfies $s<Wm$.
      - method: Construct the three-stage bank exchange on triples of a 100-element set, label it with nested nondegenerate subspaces, and count the label-dimension changes.
      - motivation (explicit): The number of smaller operations an edge needs is its label-dimension change, so a strict deficit in the total change is exactly what yields the saving.
      - relies on: linear-network, triple-construction
