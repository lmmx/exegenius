Convolution over $\mathcal R_r=\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ on the remaining axes is computed by transforming both operands, multiplying records pointwise and transforming back.

Arrays are indexed by $\prod_i\mathbb Z/t_i\mathbb Z$ over the $d-1$ remaining axes, with values in $\mathcal R_r$. Let $M=\prod_i t_i$ be the number of records, $T=rM$ the number of complex coefficients, and $*$ cyclic convolution. $\|\cdot\|_\infty$ is the largest coefficient modulus in the array. A disk array has norm at most $1$; a disk grid array also has parts that are multiples of $2^{-p}$. Multiplying by a power of $y$ permutes coefficients and changes signs, so it preserves the norm.

For arrays over $\mathcal R_r$ define $\mathcal M_r(f,g)=\frac{f*g}{rM}=\frac{f*g}{T}$. A fixed output coefficient averages $rM$ signed scalar products <cite data-source="paper">$rM$ signed scalar products, and $\mathcal M_r$ is a bilinear</cite>, so $\mathcal M_r$ is a bilinear contraction, $\|\mathcal M_r(f,g)\|_\infty\le\|f\|_\infty\|g\|_\infty$.

Let $F^-$, $F^+$ be the forward and opposite synthetic transforms. Each output entry is an average of images of the inputs under powers of $y$, so both are contractions <cite data-source="paper">Thus both transforms are contractions.</cite> Expansion of the finite sums gives $F^-(f*g)=M(F^-f)(F^-g)$. With $F^+F^-=I/M$ ([Discrete Fourier transform](dft.md)), it follows that $MF^+\bigl((F^-f)(F^-g)/r\bigr)=f*g/(rM)$. Write $P(u,v)=uv/r$ for the pointwise product (here $P$ is not a prefix count or a subspace). Each coefficient of $uv$ in $\mathcal R_r$ is a signed sum of $r$ products of coefficients, so $\|P(u,v)\|_\infty\le\|u\|_\infty\|v\|_\infty$, and $P$ is bilinear.

The transforms are laid out as in [Tape layout for synthetic transforms](transform-layout.md). Perform both forward transforms and retain their common $LB$ order, with $L$ the layout permutation of records (not a linear map, a loss or a chunk length) and $B$ the bit-reversal order (not a complement, a level factor or a row length). Their exact counterparts are $LBF^-$ and the opposite map $\mathcal T_+=F^+B^{-1}L^{-1}$; permuting records preserves the norm, so $\mathcal T_+$ is a contraction. With $\ell=\log_2r$ (here $\ell$ is not a depth), each procedure maps disk grid arrays to disk grid arrays with error less than $\delta=\sqrt2\ell\,2^{-p}$ <cite data-source="paper">$\sqrt2\ell\,2^{-p}$, returns disk grid coefficients in the same</cite>. Multiply the $M$ record pairs pointwise there, apply the opposite procedure to that order, and multiply by $M$. Since $LB$ permutes records and leaves polynomial suffixes intact, $P(LBu,LBv)=LBP(u,v)$, so $\mathcal T_+$ applied to the pointwise product of the stored forward outputs equals $F^+P(F^-f,F^-g)$; its scaling by $M$ is $\mathcal M_r(f,g)$. The procedure keeps the retained order of [Retained frequency order](frequency-order.md), passing the product to the opposite transform without reordering.

Each record product is formed by [Signed packing](signed-packing.md) in $O(rp\log(rp))$ time, truncated by $Q_p$ to $p$ bits of precision, which changes it by less than $\eta=\sqrt2\,2^{-p}$ (a truncation error bound, not a relative deficit) without raising the norm. There are $M=T/r$ products, so their total cost is $O(Tp\log(rp))$,. Each transform, and the final linear scaling, costs $O(Tp[dK+pK^{\tau-1}+\ell d^{\lambda'}])$, with $K$ the layout chunk width (not a tensor line or a segment spacing) and $\tau,\lambda'$ exponents from the cost lemma.

Let $u,v$ be the exact stored forward outputs and $\widetilde u,\widetilde v$ the returned ones, all disk arrays. By bilinearity, $P(\widetilde u,\widetilde v)-P(u,v)=P(\widetilde u-u,\widetilde v)+P(u,\widetilde v-v)$. By the contraction bound for $P$, and norm at most $1$ of $\widetilde v$ and $u$, each term is less than $\delta$, so $\|P(\widetilde u,\widetilde v)-P(u,v)\|_\infty<2\delta$. With $\widetilde z=Q_p(P(\widetilde u,\widetilde v))$ and $z_*=P(u,v)$, truncation gives $\|\widetilde z-z_*\|_\infty<2\delta+\eta$, and $\widetilde z$ is a disk grid array. The opposite procedure returns $\widetilde h$ with $\|\widetilde h-\mathcal T_+\widetilde z\|_\infty<\delta$, and $\|\mathcal T_+(\widetilde z-z_*)\|_\infty\le\|\widetilde z-z_*\|_\infty$ since $\mathcal T_+$ is a contraction. The triangle inequality gives $\|\widetilde h-\mathcal T_+z_*\|_\infty<3\delta+\eta$. The exact multiplication by $M$ gives total error less than $M(3\delta+\eta)=\sqrt2\,M(3\ell+1)2^{-p}$ <cite data-source="paper">$M(3\delta+\eta)=\sqrt2\,M(3\ell+1)2^{-p}$, as claimed.</cite>
