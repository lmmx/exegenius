## This page

Concept: Contractions and bilinear contractions
Gloss: A linear map that never increases the norm of a vector, and a bilinear map whose output norm is at most the product of the input norms, so errors in the inputs do not grow beyond a controlled bound.
Also called: contraction, bilinear contraction
