## This page

Concept: Tensor products of subspaces
Gloss: The space built from two spaces whose dimension is the product of their dimensions, in which operators and bilinear forms act factorwise.
Also called: tensor product of subspaces, tensor product
## Where the argument relies on this page

- Attaching to the gates nondegenerate subspaces of an $m$-dimensional space, nested along every edge, the number of smaller calls (the total rank of the edge changes) is $s_{\rm b}=W_{\rm b}m-N+2L_{\rm b}$ for the bit network and $s_{\rm c}=W_{\rm c}m-2N+2L_{\rm c}$ for the complex network, both strictly less than $Wm$, because $L/N<1/2$.
