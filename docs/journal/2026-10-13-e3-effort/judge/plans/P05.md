  - motivation (explicit): Multiplication reduces to convolution of short digit lists, which Fourier-type transforms compute, but their $\Theta(\log n)$ levels each scanning $\Theta(n)$ bits already cost $\Theta(n\log n)$, so the transforms themselves must be made cheaper.
