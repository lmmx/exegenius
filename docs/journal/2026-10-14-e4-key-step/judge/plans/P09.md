# Plan P09

The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): A single deterministic fixed-tape Turing machine multiplies two $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.
  - method: Build two fast tape procedures, use them for synthetic transforms and convolution over the polynomial ring, reach that ring from the integer product by Gaussian resampling with the permutations retained, then assemble the algorithm, prove exact recovery and add up the costs
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning an array of $\Theta(n)$ bits at each of the $\Theta(\log n)$ transform levels already costs $\Theta(n\log n)$, so both address rearrangement and the arithmetic of the butterfly layers must be made cheaper.
  - relies on: multitape-model, nlogn-conjecture, schoolbook-multiplication-and-karatsuba, big-o
  - **S1** (show, page `synthetic-convolution`): Normalized cyclic convolution $\mathcal M_r(f,g)=(f*g)/T$ of disk-grid arrays over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ is computed with error below $\sqrt2\,M(3\ell+1)2^{-p}$ in time $O(Tp[dK+pK^{\tau-1}+\ell d^{\lambda'}+\log(rp)])$.
    - method: Transform both factors with forward synthetic transforms that keep their frequency order, multiply the records pointwise by signed packing, and apply the opposite transform to the same order
    - motivation (explicit): The convolution theorem turns convolution into a pointwise product, and the synthetic transforms are cheap on the retained layout, so the work is to apply the simultaneous layers on a suitable layout and keep the frequency order through the product.
    - relies on: cyclic-convolution, transform-layout, signed-packing, multidim-dft, contraction-error-budget, disk-grid-truncation, polynomial-ring-negacyclic
