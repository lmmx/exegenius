The total time bound, which bounds the cost of [The multiplication algorithm](multiplication-algorithm.md), is the sum of its stage costs. Here $n$ is the bit length of each input. The power saving $\kappa$ is the exponent of the log factor $(\lg n)^\kappa$ that the bound saves below $n\lg n$.

$p=6\lceil\log_2 n\rceil$ is the coefficient precision in bits. $T\approx n/\lceil\log_2 n\rceil$ is the transform size, the number of coefficients. Its volume $V=Tp=\Theta(n)$ counts stored bits up to a constant.

The total time bound is set by [Parameter choices for the padded box](parameter-choice.md), which fixes the number of axes $d$ and the chunk width $K$ in bits. Here $\epsilon$ is the exponent in $d=\Theta(p^\epsilon)$, $c$ the exponent in $K=\lfloor d^c\rfloor$, and $\ell=\log_2 r$ the number of address bits along an axis. $\tau<1$ and $\lambda'<1$ are the chunk-exchange and simultaneous-layer exponents, $\delta$ is a small slack exponent, and $\alpha$ is the Gaussian width parameter.

Each stage cost is a power of $p$ times $V$, so exponents decide the total. Write $g_i=1-e_i$ for the margin below one of the $i$-th power $p^{e_i}$. Since $d=\Theta(p^\epsilon)$, $K=\Theta(p^{\epsilon c})$, $\ell=\Theta(p^{1-\epsilon})$ and $\alpha=\Theta(p^{1/4+\epsilon/2})$, the seven rows are $dK=p^{\epsilon(1+c)}$, $pK^{\tau-1}=p^{1-\epsilon c(1-\tau)}$, $\ell d^{\lambda'}=p^{1-\epsilon(1-\lambda')}$, $d^2\ell^\tau=p^{\tau+\epsilon(2-\tau)}$, $dp^{1/2+\delta}\alpha=p^{3/4+\delta+3\epsilon/2}$, $dp^\delta=p^{\epsilon+\delta}$ and $\log(rp)=O(p^{1-\epsilon})$.

With the fixed values $\epsilon=2^{-75}$, $c=2^{-56}$, $1-\tau=2^{-50}$, $1-\lambda'=2^{-54}$ and $\delta=1/16$, the margins are $g_2=\epsilon c(1-\tau)=2^{-181}$, $g_3=\epsilon(1-\lambda')=2^{-129}$ and $g_7=\epsilon=2^{-75}$, while the other margins exceed $2^{-51}$. So $\min_i g_i=2^{-181}=2\kappa$ <cite data-source="paper">\min_i g_i=2^{-181}=2\kappa</cite>.

Each row is bounded by an earlier cost, divided by $V$. The chunk-exchange row is based on [Fast address-chunk interchange](address-chunk-interchange.md), which swaps equal-width chunks in $O(VK^\tau)$ time; $O(p/K)$ swaps give $O(pK^{\tau-1})$. The simultaneous-layer row is costed by [Simultaneous normalized butterfly layer](simultaneous-butterfly-layer.md) at $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ per round. Axis moves cost $O(d^2)$ times $O(V(1+\ell^\tau))$. The Gaussian row sums the line costs of [Tensor resampling on a padded box](tensor-resampling.md) over the $d$ axes. The total time bound follows from [Convolution from the retained transform layout](synthetic-convolution.md), whose packed products cost $O(Tp\log(rp))$.

Each power is at most $1-2\kappa$, since each margin is at least $2\kappa$. Any fixed power of $\log p$ is at most $p^\kappa$ eventually, so each row is $O(p^{1-\kappa})$. Hence the total is $O(Vp^{1-\kappa})=O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$, since $V=\Theta(n)$ and $p=\Theta(\lg n)$.

The smallest margin comes from the chunk-exchange row, a product of three small factors. Each row must fit under $1-2\kappa$, so $\kappa$ is at most half the smallest margin; the paper takes $\kappa=2^{-182}$ <cite data-source="paper">\kappa=2^{-182}</cite>, which puts that row exactly at $1-2\kappa$.
