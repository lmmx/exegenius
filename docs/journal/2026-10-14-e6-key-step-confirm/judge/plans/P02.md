The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): A single deterministic fixed-tape Turing machine multiplies two $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.
  - method: Start from the Gaussian-resampling reduction of integer multiplication to convolution; replace the two costly operations of the synthetic transforms (address rearrangement and butterfly layers) by faster procedures built from one recursive linear-network exchange, assemble the exact algorithm, and account for its cost.
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning the whole array at each of the logarithmically many transform levels already costs $n\log n$, so both movement and arithmetic must be sped up.
  - relies on: n-log-n-conjecture, fixed-tape-model, nlogn-subroutine, big-o, turing-machine-tapes, schoolbook-multiplication-and-divide-and-conquer-multiplication
