# Plan P18

The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): A deterministic fixed-tape Turing machine multiplies two $n$-bit integers in time $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ with $\kappa=2^{-182}$, with $p$ bits of working precision, $d$ transform axes of length about $r$.
  - method: Reduce the product to a convolution through a chain of representation changes, evaluate it with the two fast tape procedures, then recover the exact product and sum the costs
  - motivation (explicit): Multiplication is a convolution of digits, convolution is computed by transforms, and the cost of transforms is dominated by address movement and butterflies, so a power saving in those two procedures gives a power saving in the whole product.
  - relies on: nlogn-conjecture, multitape-model, convolution, dft-roots-of-unity, poly-ring-yr1, butterfly, synthetic-transforms, complex-fixed-point, big-o, binary-representation-of-integers, logarithms-and-floor-ceiling, turing-machine
  - **S1** (infer, page `radix-reduction`): Splitting the inputs into radix digits reduces multiplication to convolution of short integer coefficients.
    - relies on: convolution, positional-radix-and-digit-expansion, binary-representation-of-integers
    - quotes line 76: "Splitting the inputs into radix digits reduces multiplication to"
  - **S2** (show): Two tape procedures, the address interchange and the simultaneous butterfly layer, cost $O(Vu^\tau)$ and $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$ for fixed $\tau,\lambda'<1$ on stored bit volume $V$.
    - method: Obtain both procedures from fixed linear networks with a strict rank saving and recurse
    - motivation (explicit): Both the movement of data and the arithmetic of a transform layer must be cheaper than a full scan per level, and both can be expressed as a fixed linear network exchanging banks of values.
    - **S2.1** (show, page `address-interchange`): Two disjoint contiguous address fields of $u$ bits in a Cartesian-product array of volume $V$ can be interchanged in time $O(Vu^\tau)$ for a fixed $\tau<1$.
