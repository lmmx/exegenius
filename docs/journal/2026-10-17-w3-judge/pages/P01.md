The multiplication algorithm is the reduction chain that turns the product of two $n$-bit integers into convolutions fast tape procedures evaluate, then recovers the exact $2n$-bit product; each reduction reshapes the problem for the next tool.

The algorithm takes its sizes from [Parameter choices and prime source lengths](parameter-choice.md): $b$-bit digits with $b=\lceil\log_2n\rceil$, $p=6b$ fractional bits, and $d$ axes, axis $i$ having power-of-two length $t_i\in\{r/2,r\}$ and prime length $s_i<t_i$; box sizes $T=\prod_it_i$ and $S=\prod_is_i\in(T/2,T)$.

It reduces the product by [Integer product as digit convolution](radix-digit-convolution.md) to a length-$S$ cyclic convolution of digit sequences, giving the product's coefficients $c_j<2^{3b}$. It then moves the convolution onto the prime axes by the [Chinese-remainder address map](crt-address-map.md) $\Phi$; coprimality excludes power-of-two lengths, which fast transforms need.

[Gaussian resampling](gaussian-resampling.md) expresses prime-length transforms through power-of-two ones, $RF_{\boldsymbol s}=2^\gamma\mathcal B_0QF_{\boldsymbol t}\mathcal A$, where $F_{\boldsymbol s},F_{\boldsymbol t}$ are normalized negative-sign DFTs on the two boxes, $\mathcal A,\mathcal B_0$ Gaussian contractions, $2^\gamma$ a known scale, and $R,Q$ coordinate permutations, never executed. The [Bluestein chirp identity](bluestein-chirp.md) absorbs the permutation $Q$: $QF_{\boldsymbol t}g=\overline a\cdot(a*(\overline a\cdot g))/T$ for power-of-two arrays $g$, with chirp entries $a_{\boldsymbol j}=\exp(-\pi i\sum_is_ij_i^2/t_i)=e^{\pi iN/r}$ for integer $N=-\sum_is_ij_i^2(r/t_i)\bmod2r$ ($r/t_i\in\{1,2\}$), formed by the established multiplier. The computed convolution $\widetilde{\mathcal M}_{\mathbb C}(f,g)\approx(f*g)/T$ twists the last axis into $\mathcal R_r$ by the [Coefficient (phase) twist](phase-twist.md), where twiddles $y^k$ ($y^r=-1$) are signed shifts, then convolves by [Convolution over the polynomial ring](ring-convolution.md). With $Q_p$ truncating to $p$ fractional bits and tildes marking computed maps, a prime-box array $u$ has source transform
$$\widetilde{\mathscr F}(u)=2^\gamma\widetilde{\mathcal B}_0\,Q_p\bigl(\overline{\widetilde a}\cdot\widetilde{\mathcal M}_{\mathbb C}(\widetilde a,Q_p(\overline{\widetilde a}\cdot\widetilde{\mathcal A}u))\bigr);$$
exactly, it is $RF_{\boldsymbol s}u$. Conjugation flips the Fourier sign and commutes with $R$, so $\widetilde{\mathscr F}^+(u)=\overline{\widetilde{\mathscr F}(\overline u)}$ approximates $RF^+_{\boldsymbol s}u$.

The algorithm stores each digit times $2^{-b-2}$ (exact, $<1/4$), pads to $S$ entries, applies $\Phi$, giving source arrays $u,v$, computes
$$z=Q_p\bigl(\widetilde{\mathscr F}(u)\cdot\widetilde{\mathscr F}(v)\bigr),\qquad\widetilde w=S\,\widetilde{\mathscr F}^+(z),\qquad\Phi^{-1}\operatorname{round}\bigl(2^{2b+4}S\,\widetilde w\bigr),$$
then propagates base-$2^b$ carries into $2n$ bits. It cancels the permutation $R$ by [Retained permutations and their cancellation](permutation-cancellation.md): exactly, $RF^+_{\boldsymbol s}[(RF_{\boldsymbol s}u)\cdot(RF_{\boldsymbol s}v)]=(u*v)/S^2$, so $\widetilde w$ approximates $(u*v)/S$ and the last scale gives $2^{2b+4}(u*v)$, the digit convolution, since each digit carried $2^{-b-2}$.

Errors (units of $2^{-p}$) add while arrays stay in the unit disk (norms $.5\to.51\to.701\to.501$): contractions pass errors on, a bilinear contraction $\mathcal M$ obeys $\|\mathcal M(f',g')-\mathcal M(f,g)\|\le\|f'-f\|\|g'\|+\|f\|\|g'-g\|$, and an approximate phase product adds below $4$. With $L=\log_2T$,
$$\tfrac Tr(3L+2)<5TL,\quad 5TL+4+4+4<6TL,\quad 6TL+2+4+4<8TL,\quad 2^\gamma(2dp^2+8TL)<9\cdot2^\gamma TL$$
bound the ring convolution (three transforms, truncation), $\widetilde{\mathcal M}_{\mathbb C}$ (twists), the chirp (kernel, phases) and $\widetilde{\mathscr F}$ (Gaussian maps, $2dp^2<TL$) <cite data-source="paper">E_s=9\,2^\gamma TL,</cite>.

[Rounding and carry propagation](exact-recovery.md) bounds the final error by $448b\,2^{-3b/4}<1/2$, so rounding recovers the exact coefficients $c_j$ <cite data-source="paper">\le448b\,2^{-3b/4}<\frac12.</cite>.
