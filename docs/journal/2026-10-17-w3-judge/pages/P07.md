The multiplication algorithm is the chain of reductions from two $n$-bit integers to their $2n$-bit product.

**The chain.** Parameters are set as in [Parameter choices and prime source lengths](parameter-choice.md): $b$ is the number of bits in a digit, $p=6b$ the number of fractional bits, $d$ the number of axes, $T$ the padded coefficient count, $s_i$ the odd prime length of axis $i$ with $S=\prod_i s_i$ and $T/2<S<T$, and $t_i\in\{r/2,r\}$ the power of two above $s_i$. The product reduces to a wraparound-free cyclic convolution of base-$2^b$ digits ([Integer product as digit convolution](radix-digit-convolution.md)). It is mapped to a convolution on $d$ prime axes by the [Chinese-remainder address map](crt-address-map.md) $\Phi$. Each prime-length transform is reduced to a power-of-two one with [Gaussian resampling](gaussian-resampling.md). Permutations stay in the formulas and cancel unsorted, as in [Retained permutations and their cancellation](permutation-cancellation.md). A power-of-two transform is reduced to a chirp-weighted convolution with the [Bluestein chirp identity](bluestein-chirp.md). Its last axis is moved into $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ by the [Coefficient (phase) twist](phase-twist.md). There the convolution is computed by [Convolution over the polynomial ring](ring-convolution.md), with signed shifts as roots of unity.

**Chirp and source transforms.** The chirp phase at address $j$ is computed modulo $2r$ as $-\sum_is_ij_i^2(r/t_i)$ by the established multiplier, in time $O(Tdp^{1+\delta})$ <cite data-source="paper">-\sum_i s_i j_i^2(r/t_i)\pmod{2r}.</cite>. With computed kernel $\widetilde a$, computed convolution $\widetilde{\mathcal M}_{\mathbb C}$ and truncation $Q_p$, the chirp transform forms $x_0=Q_p(\overline{\widetilde a}\cdot x)$, $y_0=\widetilde{\mathcal M}_{\mathbb C}(\widetilde a,x_0)$ and $\widetilde{\mathscr C}(x)=Q_p(\overline{\widetilde a}\cdot y_0)$. Exactly it is $QF_{\boldsymbol t}x$. The source transform $\widetilde{\mathscr F}=2^\gamma\mathcal B_0\widetilde{\mathscr C}\mathcal A$ resamples ($\mathcal A$), applies the chirp, compresses ($\mathcal B_0$), scales; exactly, it is $2^\gamma\mathcal B_0QF_{\boldsymbol t}\mathcal A=RF_{\boldsymbol s}$. Conjugating gives the opposite sign, $\widetilde{\mathscr F}^{+}(z)=\overline{\widetilde{\mathscr F}(\overline z)}$.

**Algorithm.** (1) Apply $\Phi$, giving $u,v$. (2) Compute $\widetilde{\mathscr F}(u),\widetilde{\mathscr F}(v)$. (3) Multiply pointwise, giving $z$. (4) Compute $\widetilde{\mathscr F}^{+}(z)$ times $S$, giving $\widetilde w$. (5) Multiply by $2^{2b+4}S$, round, apply $\Phi^{-1}$. (6) Propagate carries. The exact result after three transforms is $(u*v)/S^2$; the scales leave $(u*v)/S$, then $2^{2b+4}(u*v)$.

**Errors.** With $L=\log_2T$, in units of $2^{-p}$, inserting the exact bilinear map at each computed input adds input errors to its own. The ring convolution has error below $5TL$ <cite data-source="paper">E_{\rm ring}:=M(3L+2)<5TL.</cite>. Twists add $4+4+4$, giving $6TL$ for the complex convolution; kernel and phase errors $2+4+4$ give $8TL$ for the chirp <cite data-source="paper"><(6TL+2+4+4)h_p<8TLh_p.</cite>. Resampling adds $2dp^2<TL$; the source transform is within $9\cdot2^\gamma TL$ <cite data-source="paper">E_s=9\,2^\gamma TL,</cite>. Norms stay in the disk; integers are recovered by [Rounding and carry propagation](exact-recovery.md).
