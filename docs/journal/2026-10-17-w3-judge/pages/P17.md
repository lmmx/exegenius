Convolution over the polynomial ring computes the normalized [Cyclic convolution](cyclic-convolution.md) of arrays with entries in $\mathcal R_r=\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ for the multiplication algorithm's chirp step.

The arrays have $d-1$ axes; axis $i$ has length $t_i\in\{2^{\ell-1},2^\ell\}$ for $\ell=\log_2 r.$ An array has $M=\prod_i t_i$ records and $T=rM$ coefficients, in the unit disk on the grid $2^{-p}\mathbb Z[i]$ of $p$ fractional bits. $Q_p$ truncates onto this grid, and $d\ell=O(p).$

It computes its transforms by the [Synthetic polynomial transform](synthetic-transform.md): the forward and opposite transforms $F^-_{\boldsymbol t}$ and $F^+_{\boldsymbol t}$ along all axes are contractions with $F^+_{\boldsymbol t}F^-_{\boldsymbol t}=I/M$, and $B$ is their bit-reversed storage order. For arrays $f,g$ we have $F^-_{\boldsymbol t}(f*g)=M(F^-_{\boldsymbol t}f)(F^-_{\boldsymbol t}g),$ so with $P$ the recordwise product divided by $r$ the target satisfies
$$\mathcal M_r(f,g)=\frac{f*g}{T}=M\,F^+_{\boldsymbol t}\,P(F^-_{\boldsymbol t}f,\,F^-_{\boldsymbol t}g).$$

The tape procedures compute $LBF^-_{\boldsymbol t}$ and $F^+_{\boldsymbol t}B^{-1}L^{-1}$ with $L$ a layout permutation of addresses. Since $P(LBF^-_{\boldsymbol t}f,LBF^-_{\boldsymbol t}g)=LB\,P(F^-_{\boldsymbol t}f,F^-_{\boldsymbol t}g)$ forward outputs are multiplied in place and the opposite procedure cancels their order. It computes its pointwise products by [Signed packing of polynomial products](signed-packing.md) in $O(rp\log(rp))$ per pair.

A round on index bit $h$ applies butterflies, then twiddle monomials $y^E.$ It runs most rounds as calls of the [Fast simultaneous butterfly layer](fast-butterfly-layer.md), $O(Tpd^{\lambda'})$ each with fixed $\lambda'<1$, if bit $h$ of all axes sits at one offset in adjacent address chunks of $K\le\ell-1$ bits; $L$ arranges this.

1. For $b=(\ell-1)\bmod K$ and $q=\lfloor(\ell-1)/K\rfloor$ move the top bit of each length-$2^\ell$ axis and bits $b-1,\dots,0$ of all axes to the front by $(d-1)(b+1)=O(dK)$ moves of cost $O(Tp)$; bits $\ell-2,\dots,b$ remain as $q$ chunks per axis.
2. It exchanges chunks by the [Fast address-chunk interchange](address-chunk-interchange.md), $O(TpK^\tau)$ each with fixed $\tau<1$, until equal indices are adjacent, at most $(d-1)q-1=O(p/K)$ exchanges costing $O(Tp\,pK^{\tau-1}).$
3. Bit $h$ then lies at offset $(h-b)\bmod K$ in equal-index chunks: one layer call per chunk round. The at most $b+1\le K$ front rounds cost $O(TpdK)$ bit by bit, exact in $p+d+O(1)=O(p)$ bits until one $Q_p.$

A transform thus costs $O\big(Tp[dK+pK^{\tau-1}+\ell d^{\lambda'}]\big).$ Truncation toward zero is odd per component and so commutes with the signed coefficient shift $y^E$: each round is an exact contraction then one truncation, so at most $\ell$ rounds err by under $\sqrt2\,\ell\,2^{-p}.$ Relocating slots conjugates rounds by $L$, applied first or undone last:
$$(LBF^-_{\boldsymbol t}L^{-1})L=LBF^-_{\boldsymbol t},\qquad L^{-1}(LF^+_{\boldsymbol t}B^{-1}L^{-1})=F^+_{\boldsymbol t}B^{-1}L^{-1}.$$
