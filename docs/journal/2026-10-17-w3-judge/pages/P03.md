Convolution over the polynomial ring computes the normalized cyclic convolution of arrays with entries in $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$.

Take $d-1$ axes ($d$ free) of lengths $t_i\in\{2^{\ell-1},2^\ell\}$, with $\ell=\log_2 r$. Let $M=\prod_i t_i$ count the records, $T=rM$ the complex coefficients, $p$ the fractional bits kept per coefficient, $K=\lfloor d^c\rfloor$ the bit-width of an address chunk, and $\tau,\lambda'<1$ the exponents of the chunk swap and butterfly layer.

$\mathcal M_r(f,g)=(f*g)/T$ is the normalization of the [cyclic convolution](cyclic-convolution.md) $f*g$ on these axes. Each output coefficient averages $rM$ signed products <cite data-source="paper">$rM$ signed scalar products, and $\mathcal M_r$ is a bilinear</cite>, so $\|\mathcal M_r(f,g)\|_\infty\le\|f\|_\infty\|g\|_\infty$.

The fast layer needs equal-offset bits in consecutive chunks. Put $b=(\ell-1)\bmod K$ and $q=\lfloor(\ell-1)/K\rfloor$: leftover bits and whole chunks. Moving to a prefix the top bit of each long axis and bits $b-1,\dots,0$ of all axes touches at most $(d-1)(b+1)=O(dK)$ bits, costing $O(TpdK)$. Reordering the remaining chunks takes at most $(d-1)q-1$ exchanges by the [fast address-chunk interchange](address-chunk-interchange.md), each $O(TpK^\tau)$; as $(d-1)q=O(p/K)$, the total is $O(Tp\cdot pK^{\tau-1})$. The first $b+1\le K$ rounds then act on prefix bits singly, costing $O(TpdK)$. The other $qK$ rounds see every address combination, so each costs $O(Tpd^{\lambda'})$ by the [fast simultaneous butterfly layer](fast-butterfly-layer.md), and $\ell$ rounds total $O(Tp\ell d^{\lambda'})$. Truncation toward zero is odd and the monomial step $J_E$ is a signed coefficient permutation, so $J_EQ_p(\mathcal Hz)=Q_p(J_E\mathcal Hz)$ for the parallel butterfly $\mathcal H$: each round is one truncation after an exact contraction. Contractions keep error, each truncation adds less than $\sqrt2\,2^{-p}$, and the moves add none, so $\ell$ rounds give error below $\sqrt2\ell\,2^{-p}$. Let $L$ be the permutation made by the moves and $B$ the bit reversal left by the rounds; the exact forward and opposite operators are $LBF^-_{\boldsymbol t}$ and $F^+_{\boldsymbol t}B^{-1}L^{-1}$.

The convolution is computed through the [synthetic transform](synthetic-transform.md), where $F^-_{\boldsymbol t}(f*g)=M(F^-_{\boldsymbol t}f)(F^-_{\boldsymbol t}g)$ and $F^+_{\boldsymbol t}F^-_{\boldsymbol t}=I/M$. Hence $MF^+_{\boldsymbol t}\big((F^-_{\boldsymbol t}f)(F^-_{\boldsymbol t}g)/r\big)=\mathcal M_r(f,g)$. A record permutation commutes with the pointwise product $P(u,v)=uv/r$, so forward outputs are multiplied in stored order and fed directly to the opposite transform. Pointwise products in $\mathcal R_r$ are formed by [signed packing](signed-packing.md), returning $Q_p(uv/r)$, truncated to multiples of $2^{-p}$. Put $\delta=\sqrt2\ell\,2^{-p}$ and $\eta=\sqrt2\,2^{-p}$, bounds on transform and truncation error. As $P$ is a bilinear contraction, forward outputs within $\delta$ of exact give products within $2\delta$, and truncating gives $2\delta+\eta$. The contraction $F^+_{\boldsymbol t}B^{-1}L^{-1}$ adds error below $\delta$, so the output is within $3\delta+\eta$. Scaling by $M$ leaves error below $\sqrt2\,M(3\ell+1)2^{-p}$ <cite data-source="paper">$M(3\delta+\eta)=\sqrt2\,M(3\ell+1)2^{-p}$, as claimed.</cite>

The $M$ pointwise products <cite data-source="paper">There are $M=T/r$ pointwise polynomial products.</cite> cost $O(Tp\log(rp))$; with three transforms the total is $O(Tp[dK+pK^{\tau-1}+\ell d^{\lambda'}+\log(rp)])$.
