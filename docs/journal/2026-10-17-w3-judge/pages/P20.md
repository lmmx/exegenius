Packed selected-bit addition XORs bit $j_i$ of chunk $x$ into bit $j_i$ of chunk $y$ at all selected positions, restoring scratch chunk $z.$ The chunks have $L=fK$ bits, and $f$ counts the positions $j_i=\rho+iK$ $(0\le i<f,$ $0\le\rho<K)$ of segment width $K.$ It uses a few whole-chunk cyclic shifts, then a correction at few addresses <cite data-source="paper">The construction first uses a constant number of cyclic shifts of whole chunk ranges, then corrects an explicitly bounded set of addresses.</cite>

For Boolean $c$ and integers $u,w,$ apply in order, testing current values:
$$u\mathrel{-}=c[w\equiv0\pmod2],\ w\mathrel{-}=c[u\equiv0\pmod2],\ u\mathrel{+}=c[w\equiv0\pmod2],\ w\mathrel{+}=c[u\equiv0\pmod2],$$
$$w\mathrel{+}=c[u\equiv1\pmod2],\ u\mathrel{+}=c[w\equiv1\pmod2],\ w\mathrel{-}=c[u\equiv1\pmod2],\ u\mathrel{-}=c[w\equiv1\pmod2].$$
If $c=1,$ substituting the parity pairs of $(u,w)$ shows the first four lines induce the cycle $(00\ 01\ 10)$ <cite data-source="paper">induce the cycle $(00\ 01\ 10)$ in every square</cite> and the last four induce $(01\ 10\ 11).$ Applying the first cycle and then the second gives $(00\ 10)(01\ 11),$ since $00\to01\to10,$ $01\to10\to11,$ $10\to00\to00,$ $11\to11\to01.$ So $(u',w')=(u+c(1-2(u\bmod2)),w):$ the parity of $u$ flips exactly when $c=1,$ both quotients by two are preserved, $w$ is restored, and each coordinate moves by at most eight.

For $0\le i<f-1$ (top position set aside) write the $K$-bit segments of $y,z$ at $j_i$ as $u_i=2g_i+a_i,$ $w_i=2h_i+b_i$ with $a_i,b_i\in\{0,1\},$ guard values $g_i,h_i\in\{0,\dots,2^{K-1}-1\},$ and control $c_i=x_{j_i}.$ One line for all segments is the cyclic shift
$$y\gets y+\varepsilon\sum_{i=0}^{f-2}2^{j_i}[x_{j_i}=1][z_{j_i}=b]\pmod{2^L},$$
with sign $\varepsilon$ and tested parity $b.$ Its offset does not depend on the target, so each line is a bijection; $S_0$ is their composition.

Let $\mathcal B$ be the addresses where some used guard fails $10\le g\le2^{K-1}-11.$ Outside $\mathcal B$ every segment lies in $[20,2^K-21]$ and even displacement eight stays within $[0,2^K-1],$ so no segment carries into the next and $S_0$ agrees with the XOR permutation $T_0.$ Each guard has exactly twenty excluded values among $2^{K-1}$ <cite data-source="paper">There are exactly twenty excluded values for each guard in</cite>, so the union bound over $2(f-1)$ guards gives $|\mathcal B|\le\delta_{\rm bad}M$ for $M$ records, independently of record values, with $\delta_{\rm bad}=\min\{1,80(f-1)2^{-K}\}.$

Since $T_0$ and $S_0$ are bijections agreeing off $\mathcal B,$ $T_0S_0^{-1}$ fixes $\mathcal B^c$ and permutes $\mathcal B,$ so the correction permutes the exceptional records.

Each rotation moves its target to the rightmost chunk position by one width-$L$ chunk swap ([Fast address-chunk interchange](address-chunk-interchange.md), $O(VL^\tau)),$ applies [Controlled cyclic shifts](controlled-shift.md) in $O(V),$ and undoes the swap; $S_0$ uses at most sixteen swaps and eight rotations <cite data-source="paper">There are at most sixteen swaps and eight rotations in $S_0$.</cite>

Extract the records of $\mathcal B$ with destination ranks as keys, stably radix-sort only those, and reinsert them in one forward scan; the keys are distinct and equal the marked holes, so this implements $T_0$ everywhere.

Total movement is $O(V(L^\tau+1)),$ scans $O(MA^3+V)$ and radix passes $O(|\mathcal B|A(R+A)),$ for $A=\lceil\log_2(2V)\rceil$ and $R$ bits per record. After dividing by $V=MR$ the extra terms are bounded by $A^3/R$ and $80(f-1)2^{-K}A(1+A/R)$; with $A=O(p),$ $R$ above every polynomial in the size parameter $p,$ $f\le d\le p$ and $K/\log p\to\infty,$ they are $o(1),$ so the cost is $O(V(L^\tau+1)).$ <cite data-source="paper">After division by $V=MR$, its additional terms are bounded by</cite> A fixed invertible binary change of basis on $e=mf$ axes (and its inverse) costs $O(V((eK)^\tau+1)),$ since slot width is $L=(e/m)K.$
