Packed selected-bit addition XORs $x_{j_i}$ into $y_{j_i}$ at the $f$ positions $j_i=\rho+iK$ ($K$ the segment width in bits) of chunks $x,y$, restoring a third chunk $z$, for the [butterfly layer](fast-butterfly-layer.md)'s changes of basis.

$L=fK$ is the chunk width in bits. With $M$ records of $R$ bits, volume is $V=MR$ and addresses have $A=\lceil\log_2(2V)\rceil$ bits.

For a Boolean control $c$ and integers $u,w$, perform in order (brackets read current values):

$$\begin{array}{llll} u\leftarrow u-c[w\bmod2=0], & w\leftarrow w-c[u\bmod2=0], & u\leftarrow u+c[w\bmod2=0], & w\leftarrow w+c[u\bmod2=0],\\ w\leftarrow w+c[u\bmod2=1], & u\leftarrow u+c[w\bmod2=1], & w\leftarrow w-c[u\bmod2=1], & u\leftarrow u-c[w\bmod2=1]. \end{array}$$

For $c=1$, substituting parity pairs of $(u,w)$ shows the first four updates cycle them as $(00\ 01\ 10)$ in every square $\{2a,2a+1\}\times\{2b,2b+1\}$ <cite data-source="paper">induce the cycle $(00\ 01\ 10)$ in every square</cite>, the last four as $(01\ 10\ 11)$. Composing them gives $(00\ 10)(01\ 11)$: $00\to01\to10$, $10\to00\to00$, $01\to10\to11$, $11\to11\to01$. Hence
$$(u',w')=\bigl(u+c(1-2(u\bmod2)),\ w\bigr):$$
the parity of $u$ flips exactly when $c=1$, while its quotient by two and $w$ are unchanged, each coordinate moving at most $8$.

Handle the top position apart in $O(V)$. For $0\le i<f-1$, the $K$-bit segments from bit $j_i$ are disjoint; write their values in $y$ and $z$ as $u_i=2g_i+a_i$ and $w_i=2h_i+b_i$, with guard values $g_i,h_i<2^{K-1}$ and control $c_i=x_{j_i}$. Each line is, for all segments together, one cyclic shift (sign $\varepsilon$, tested parity $b$)
$$y\leftarrow y+\varepsilon\sum_{i=0}^{f-2}2^{j_i}[x_{j_i}=1][z_{j_i}=b]\pmod{2^L}$$
<cite data-source="paper">\varepsilon\sum_{i=0}^{f-2}2^{j_i}</cite>. Its offset ignores the target, so each line is a bijection; $S_0$ is their composition.

Let $\mathcal B$ be the addresses where some guard fails $10\le g\le2^{K-1}-11$. Elsewhere segments lie in $[20,2^K-21]$ and displacement $8$ stays in $[0,2^K-1]$, so no segment carries into the next and $S_0$ agrees with the ideal XOR permutation $T_0$. Each guard has $20$ excluded values among $2^{K-1}$, so the union bound over $2(f-1)$ guards gives $|\mathcal B|\le\delta_{\rm bad}M$ with $\delta_{\rm bad}=\min\{1,80(f-1)2^{-K}\}$.

Since $T_0,S_0$ are bijections agreeing off $\mathcal B$, $T_0S_0^{-1}$ fixes $\mathcal B^c$ and permutes $\mathcal B$. Extract the records of $\mathcal B$ keyed by destination rank, radix-sort only them, and reinsert in one forward scan; the keys are distinct and equal the marked holes' ranks, implementing $T_0$ everywhere.

Each rotation swaps its target to the rightmost position (width $L$, $O(VL^\tau)$ by [address-chunk interchange](address-chunk-interchange.md)), shifts in $O(V)$ by [controlled shifts](controlled-shift.md), and swaps back; $S_0$ uses $\le16$ swaps and $8$ rotations. Movement costs $O(V(L^\tau+1))$ <cite data-source="paper">The movement costs $O(V(L^\tau+1))$.</cite>, full scans $O(MA^3+V)$, radix passes $O(|\mathcal B|A(R+A))$. Per unit volume the extras are $A^3/R$ and $80(f-1)2^{-K}A(1+A/R)$, both $o(1)$ when $p$, the address-length parameter, satisfies $A=O(p)$, $R$ exceeds every fixed polynomial in $p$, $f\le p$ and $K/\log p\to\infty$; the total is $O(V(L^\tau+1))$.

Let a fixed invertible $\mathbb F_2$ matrix of order $m$ act on $e=mf$ axis chunks. Slot width is $L=(e/m)K$, so change of basis and inverse cost $O(V((eK)^\tau+1))$ <cite data-source="paper">$O(V((eK)^\tau+1))$ on a stream of volume $V$.</cite>.
