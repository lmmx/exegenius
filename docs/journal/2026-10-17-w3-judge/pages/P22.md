Retained permutations are the two coordinate permutations of [Gaussian resampling](gaussian-resampling.md), kept inside the transform formula instead of being executed as sorts; this page shows that neither one costs a sort when two integers are convolved.

Let $\boldsymbol s$ be the vector of pairwise coprime source lengths $s_i$ and $\boldsymbol t$ the vector of even power-of-two target lengths $t_i$, with $\gcd(s_i,t_i)=1$. The source permutation is $R=\bigotimes_i P_{s_i}$ with $(P_{s_i}u)_j=u_{t_ij}$, and the target permutation is $Q=\bigotimes_i P_{t_i}$. The transforms $F_{\boldsymbol s}$ and $F^+_{\boldsymbol s}$ are the tensor products of the normalized [discrete Fourier transforms](dft.md) and their opposite-sign versions. Write $S=\prod_i s_i$ for the number of entries of a source array and $T=\prod_i t_i$ for the number of entries of a target array. Gaussian resampling gives $RF_{\boldsymbol s}=2^\gamma\mathcal B_0QF_{\boldsymbol t}\mathcal A$, where $\mathcal A,\mathcal B_0$ are the cheap resampling contractions and $2^\gamma$ is a scalar.

## The target permutation

The middle factor $QF_{\boldsymbol t}$ is computed without applying $Q$. It is written, by the [Bluestein chirp identity](bluestein-chirp.md), as a chirp-weighted [cyclic convolution](cyclic-convolution.md),
$$QF_{\boldsymbol t}u=\overline a\cdot\frac{a*(\overline a\cdot u)}{T},\qquad a_{\boldsymbol j}=\exp\Bigl(-\pi i\sum_i s_ij_i^2/t_i\Bigr),$$
so the frequency change in $Q$ happens inside the convolution <cite data-source="paper">in $Q$ without applying $Q$ as a separate permutation of the array.</cite>

## The source permutation

$R$ stays in both forward transforms. We show $RF^+_{\boldsymbol s}[(RF_{\boldsymbol s}u)\cdot(RF_{\boldsymbol s}v)]=(u*v)/S^2$, so the retained permutation disappears <cite data-source="paper">retained source permutation $R$ cancels when computing a convolution.</cite>

Take a unit $c$ modulo $q$, that is $\gcd(c,q)=1$, and define $(R_cz)_j=z_{cj}$ with indices modulo $q$. Substituting $h=ck$, which permutes the residues because $c$ is a unit, gives
$$(F_q^+R_cz)_j=\frac1q\sum_{k}z_{ck}e^{2\pi i kj/q}=\frac1q\sum_{h}z_he^{2\pi i h c^{-1}j/q}=(R_c^{-1}F_q^+z)_j.$$
Each $t_i$ is a unit modulo $s_i$ and $R=\bigotimes_iR_{t_i}$, so tensoring gives $F^+_{\boldsymbol s}R=R^{-1}F^+_{\boldsymbol s}$ and therefore $RF^+_{\boldsymbol s}R=F^+_{\boldsymbol s}$.

A coordinate permutation commutes with coordinatewise products, so $(RF_{\boldsymbol s}u)\cdot(RF_{\boldsymbol s}v)=R(F_{\boldsymbol s}u\cdot F_{\boldsymbol s}v)$. Hence
$$RF^+_{\boldsymbol s}\bigl[R(F_{\boldsymbol s}u\cdot F_{\boldsymbol s}v)\bigr]=F^+_{\boldsymbol s}(F_{\boldsymbol s}u\cdot F_{\boldsymbol s}v).$$
The convolution rule $F_{\boldsymbol s}(u*v)=S(F_{\boldsymbol s}u)(F_{\boldsymbol s}v)$ and $F^+_{\boldsymbol s}F_{\boldsymbol s}=I/S$ turn the right side into
$$\frac1SF^+_{\boldsymbol s}F_{\boldsymbol s}(u*v)=\frac{u*v}{S^2}.$$
Neither permutation is ever executed: $Q$ is absorbed by the chirp convolution and $R$ cancels algebraically, which leaves only the ordinary convolution $u*v$ up to the known scalar.
