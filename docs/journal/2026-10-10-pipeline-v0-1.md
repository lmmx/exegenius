## Findings during implementation

- **zensical defaults.** zensical 0.0.69 enables `pymdownx.arithmatex` (generic) by
  default, and a `[project.markdown_extensions]` table *replaces* the defaults
  (`config.get("markdown_extensions", DEFAULT_MARKDOWN_EXTENSIONS)` in zensical/config.py).
  The generated `zensical.toml` therefore leaves extensions alone and adds only the
  MathJax scripts.
- **`oneOf` in schemas.** schemars 1.2 emits `oneOf` with `const` variants for a unit enum
  with documented variants (`GapKind`). The structured-outputs schema subset lists `enum`,
  `const`, `anyOf` and `allOf`, but not `oneOf`, so the schema is rewritten before sending.
- **Stale links at build.** Page bodies are written against the plan at the time they are
  written. A later plan can drop a page, so `build` checks every body's links against the
  current plan again.

## Current State
- Cargo workspace with edition 2024 and resolver 3, crates `exegenius-core` and `exegenius-cli` (Cargo.toml, exegenius-core/Cargo.toml, exegenius-cli/Cargo.toml)
- `Config::load` parses `exegenius.toml` with unknown fields rejected and model settings defaulted (`claude-opus-5-5`, effort `high`, max_tokens 128000, fallbacks on) (exegenius-core/src/config.rs:67)
- `Project::load` reads every source file and keeps a normalised copy for quote checks (exegenius-core/src/project.rs:63)
- `normalise` maps typographic quotes and dashes to ASCII, removes soft hyphens, and collapses whitespace before any verbatim quote comparison (exegenius-core/src/text.rs:5)
- `schema_of` derives each response schema from its Rust type with schemars, removes `$schema`, numeric and length constraints and formats, closes every object with all properties required, and rewrites `oneOf` as a string `enum` or `anyOf` (exegenius-core/src/model.rs:45-67)
- `extract` makes one call over all sources, slugs ids and edges, drops quotes not found verbatim in their named source, runs `Graph::check` (duplicate ids, unknown `requires`, missing target, Tarjan SCC cycles), and makes one repair call carrying the error list before failing (exegenius-core/src/extract.rs:139, exegenius-core/src/graph.rs:113)
- `extend` sends the distinct candidate terms with up to three uses each, validates the new concepts and resolutions against the merged graph, adds each term as an alias, and turns each candidate into a prerequisite edge from its page or a rewrite note when the edge would close a cycle (exegenius-core/src/extract.rs:181, exegenius-core/src/extract.rs:275)
- `plan` takes the prerequisite closure of the target, stopping at known concepts, and topologically sorts it with ties broken by the position of each concept's first quote in the sources (exegenius-core/src/plan.rs:51)
- `Plan::known_at` gives the reader's knowledge at page i as the known concepts plus the pages before i, and both the write and audit prompts are built from it (exegenius-core/src/plan.rs:28)
- `write_pages` writes only pages with no body or queued for rewrite, and passes the rewrite notes plus the previous body back to the model (exegenius-core/src/write.rs:143)
- `fix_links` replaces links to anything other than a planned page, `index.md` or an external URL with their text, using pulldown-cmark offsets (exegenius-core/src/write.rs:196)
- `audit_pages` sends each page with the reader's knowledge at that page and no sources, then appends a round record to `exegenius/audit.json` (exegenius-core/src/audit.rs:141)
- `apply_gaps` drops a gap whose quote is not on the page or whose term the reader knows, queues rewrite notes for symbols and steps, and through `term_action` turns a term into a new edge, a link note, an inline-explanation note, or a candidate concept (exegenius-core/src/audit.rs:181, exegenius-core/src/audit.rs:242)
- `build` writes `docs/<id>.md` with the title, prerequisites ("assumed known" for known concepts), "Used by" and sources sections taken from the graph, plus `docs/index.md` and `docs/javascripts/mathjax.js` (written only if absent), and removes files listed in `exegenius/built.json` that are no longer generated (exegenius-core/src/build.rs:62)
- `zensical_toml` creates or edits `zensical.toml` with toml_edit, replacing `project.nav` with the reading order, adding the MathJax scripts to `extra_javascript`, setting `site_name` and `docs_dir` only when absent, and keeping all other content (exegenius-core/src/build.rs:224)
- `Anthropic` posts to `/v1/messages` with streaming, structured outputs, effort, `cache_control` on the sources block, and `fallbacks: "default"` for non-Haiku models; it retries 408/429/5xx/529 up to four times and caches each response under the SHA-256 of the request body in `exegenius/cache/` (exegenius-cli/src/anthropic.rs:31, exegenius-cli/src/anthropic.rs:150)
- The CLI exposes `init`, `extract [--fresh]`, `plan`, `write`, `audit`, `build`, and `run [--rounds N]` with `-C/--dir` (exegenius-cli/src/main.rs)
- `run` repeats write and audit until a round reports zero gaps or N rounds pass, then resolves candidates, writes the last fixes without auditing them, and builds (exegenius-cli/src/main.rs)
- The Justfile provides `check`, `clippy`, `fmt`, `test`, `precommit`, `prepush`, `install`, and `docs`, and .pre-commit-config.yaml runs `just precommit` plus conventional-commit message checks (Justfile, .pre-commit-config.yaml)
