The plan of the argument for the main theorem, as a tree of steps.

- **S0** (show, page `main-theorem`): A single deterministic fixed-tape Turing machine multiplies two $n$-bit integers exactly in $O(n(\lg n)^{1-\kappa})$ worst-case time with $\kappa=2^{-182}$.
  - method: Reduce multiplication to a convolution over $\mathbb C[y]/(y^r+1)$ through Gaussian resampling, speed up the synthetic transforms with two tape procedures that each save a power of the chunk width (fast address-chunk interchange and simultaneous butterfly layers), and account for precision and cost
  - motivation (explicit): Scanning $\Theta(n)$ bits at each of $\Theta(\log n)$ transform levels already costs $n\log n$, so both data movement and arithmetic must be sped up by a power of the width.
