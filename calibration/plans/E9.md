# E9 (preregistered): a sentence asking for parameter definitions and every case

**Status:** written and committed before any E9 run (runbook item 4c). The pipeline default is unchanged. The E8 sentence stays proposed to the author whatever E9 shows.

## The decision it informs

**Whether the instruction proposal to the author should carry a second sentence.**

The one central item Sonnet plans still miss is C1, and the cause looks like selection rather than reading.

- **Every central miss is C1.** In J9, J10, J12 and J16, every plan with a central count of 8 misses C1, which is "mentioned" by majority:
  - E7F1W, E7F4W, E7F8W (J9, J10, J16);
  - S2F1SX (J9);
  - S3F4OH (J10);
  - E8F4X, E8F8X (J16).
- **It depends on the extraction.** In J8 (E7), the W plans reconstruct C1 on F1, F3, F5, F6 and F7, and miss it on F2, F4 and F8. E6's V plans miss it on F2, F4, F5, F6 and F8.
- **The plans that reconstruct C1 come from stronger configurations or the sentence on F1:** S2F1OX, S2F1OM, S2F1SM, S3F4OX, S3F8OX, S4F8OM, S5F8OX and E8F1X.
- **C1 is the parameter setup:** $h=100$, the data pairs indexed by triples, $N=\binom h3^3$ and $m=h^3$ (`rubrics/nlogn-central-saving.md`).
  - J16's judge on E8F4X: "$h=100$ … $N$ and $m$ are never given; $N$ and $m$ appear undefined in other steps."
- **The C1-missing plans do not define the parameters** (judge-free check). Of E8F1X, E8F4X, E8F8X, S3F8OX, S4F8OM and E7F8W, only the C1-reconstructing ones (E8F1X, S3F8OX, S4F8OM) state $h=100$ or $m=h^3$ in any node.
- **It is not a reading gap.** Every argument run of E8 and E8b read the paper's C1 region (lines 640–660) and the regions of the checklist sub-steps that X plans still miss most. So did the E7 runs and S3F4OX and S3F8OX, except that F7W and F8W did not read S1's region:
  - L1 and L2: the per-edge residual table;
  - S1: the constants;
  - M2: the avoiding prime;
  - R2 and R3: the exact constants.
  
  The check counted `Read` and `sed` ranges in the transcripts. The ranges are coarse (whole chunks), so "read" means the material entered the planner's context. The planners saw the material and did not carry it.
- **What X plans still miss on the checklist** (J15 and J17, read together only to locate gaps, not to compare):
  - L1 in 8 of 9 X plans;
  - L2 in 8 of 9;
  - M2 in 6 of 9;
  - S1 in at least one judge's verdict on 6 of 9.
  - J16's judges also note that "the gate-label table itself is not given" (C3).

**The hypothesis:** a generic sentence asking:
- for each fixed parameter and constant to be defined, with its value;
- and for every case of a case-by-case or tabulated step;

closes C1 and lifts L1, L2 and S1, at no cost to the primary measures.

**The sentence,** appended to E8's key-step instruction on branch `141026-exp-params-cases` (e8efaa3). It does not name this paper:

> Define each parameter and constant the target result's statement or derivation fixes (a size, a count, a threshold) with the value the sources give it, at the step that first uses it; where the sources establish a sub-step case by case or by a table, give every case and what it contributes.

## Reading (fixed now)

**Beyond judge noise:** ranges across judges do not overlap.

**Y closes C1** if Y's C1 is reconstructed by majority on at least 2 of 3 extractions, and on more extractions than X's is.

**No loss** if, on every extraction:
- Y's gate passes by majority;
- Y's faithful range does not lie entirely below X's;
- Y has no more major errors than X (by median);
- and Y's checklist range does not lie entirely below X's.

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| closes C1, no loss | the second sentence is added to the proposal to the author, as a separate item from the E8 sentence |
| closes C1, a loss on some extraction | reported. Not proposed; the loss is examined first |
| does not close C1 | the parameter gap is not closed by asking. C1 is recorded as the known remaining gap, and the proposal is the E8 sentence alone |
| X already reconstructs C1 on all three | E9 cannot show a C1 effect. It is reported as uninformative on C1, and the checklist result is reported as secondary |

**Reported, not criteria:**
- the checklist per extraction;
- L1, L2, S1 and M2 presence;
- Y's node count over X's.

Length is reported because the plans feed writers. E8's X plans were already 2–3× W's.
