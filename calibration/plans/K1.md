# K1 (preregistered): validating the mechanism checklist

**Status:** written and committed before any checklist judgement, and before the markers were run on any plan in K1's set. The author approved the validation on 2026-10-14 ("go ahead with the checklist validation").

## The decision it informs

**Whether the top of the scale can be measured.** The next comparisons are Opus `xhigh` against Opus `max`, and the best existing configuration against Fable. Both sit at the top of the faithful-derivation scale, where the current rubric has lost resolution: Opus `xhigh` 18, Sonnet `max` 18 and Opus `max` 19 on F1 cannot be separated (`docs/journal/2026-10-14-sonnet-xhigh-and-rubric.md`).

K1 decides whether the mechanism checklist (`calibration/rubrics/nlogn-mechanism-checklist.md`):
- may serve as a preregistered secondary measure in those comparisons;
- and whether it can separate plans at the top.

If it cannot, top-end comparisons on this paper are not worth running, and the next step is a harder input (a second document).

## Design

**Plans:** eleven existing plans, with no new runs.
- J9's five on F1: E7F1W, S2F1SX, S2F1OX, S2F1SM, S2F1OM.
- J10's six on F4 and F8: E7F4W, E7F8W, S3F4OH, S3F4OX, S3F8OH, S3F8OX.

**Judging, batch J11:**
- **Blinding:** the plans are rendered blind by `tools/prepare.py`, seed 20261025.
- **Judges:** one checklist judgement per plan, plus a second on each of the three F1 plans at the top (S2F1OX, S2F1SM, S2F1OM). That makes 14 judgements.
- **Configuration:** `general-purpose`, `model: "opus"`, as in J9 and J10.
- **The task:** the judge gets only the checklist, the paper and the plan. Not the reference pack, not the earlier verdicts.

**Markers:** `tools/markers.py`, run on the same eleven plans; free.

## Criteria (fixed now)

1. **Validity against the existing rubric.**
   - **The pairs:** for each of the 66 pairs of a plan and one of the six core results:
     - the result's completeness (sub-steps judged present, over its sub-steps);
     - set against whether a majority of that plan's J9 or J10 judges called the result faithful.
   - **Met if:**
     - completeness is higher on average for faithful pairs;
     - and the AUC is at least 0.80. The AUC is the probability that a random faithful pair has higher completeness than a random non-faithful pair, ties counting half.
2. **Agreement with the markers:**
   - **What is compared:** on the 25 sub-steps that have a marker (275 pairs with the eleven plans), the judge's `present` against the marker.
   - **Met if** they agree on at least 0.80 of pairs.
   - **Disagreements are reported by direction:**
     - judge present, marker absent: expected where notation varies;
     - marker present, judge absent: the content may appear without its role.
3. **Judge reliability:**
   - **What is compared:** on the three double-judged plans (78 sub-step verdicts), the two judges' `present` verdicts.
   - **Met if** they agree on at least 0.80, and each plan's two totals are within 3.
4. **Resolution at the top** (reported; not a condition of adoption):
   - **The test:** whether any two of S2F1OX, S2F1SM and S2F1OM differ in total by more than the larger of their judge disagreements.
   - **Also reported:** whether the checklist keeps the orderings J10 established beyond noise:
     - S3F8OX above S3F8OH;
     - S3F4OX and S3F8OX above their references.

## Decision

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| 1, 2 and 3 met | the checklist is adopted as a preregistered secondary measure for the next experiment (Opus `max` on F8, and any Fable pilot); 4 decides whether it may break ties at the ceiling |
| 1 or 3 not met | not adopted. The checklist is revised (if the failures point to particular sub-steps) or dropped; the next experiment uses the existing rubric only |
| 2 not met, 1 and 3 met | adopted for judges; the markers are revised before being used as a cross-check |
| 4 shows no resolution | top-end comparisons on this paper's argument task are not worth their cost; the next step is a second document |

**A hold-out check, reported only:** markers against the existing faithful verdicts on J8's sixteen plans, which K1's judges never see.
