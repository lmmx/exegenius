## The decision it informs

**Whether to propose the combined instruction to the author** (the E8 and E9 sentences together, branch `141026-exp-params-cases`, e8efaa3) for the argument task on this paper.

- **E8b's stage 2 was not supported** (`plans/E8b.md`). The E8 sentence alone beat W beyond noise on F2, F3 and F7, and tied on F5 and F6. So its proposal is withdrawn by E8b's table.

**E10 asks whether Y beats W beyond noise where X did not or barely did, and how much Y varies between runs.**

## Reading (fixed now)

**Beyond judge noise:** the judges' ranges do not overlap.

**Y beats W on an extraction if all of these hold:**
- Y's faithful range is above W's;
- Y's gate passes by majority;
- Y has no more major errors than W (by median).

**Y's run variance is the difference between E9F4Y and E10F4Y2:**
- in faithful medians and in C1 by majority;
- the variance condition is met if the faithful difference is smaller than Y's smallest gain over W beyond noise.

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| Y beats W on all three (F3, F5, F6), and the variance condition is met | **propose the combined instruction to the author** for the argument task on this paper, with all the evidence: E7 → E8 → E9 → E10, and the extractions it rests on. Its generality rests on the second document. The pipeline default changes only on the author's decision |
| Y beats W on F3 only, or on one of F5 and F6 | the instructions do not lift extractions where W is already strong. No proposal; examine the per-result verdicts for what W and Y miss there |
| Y below W on any extraction (W's range above Y's) | the combined instruction is withdrawn |
| the variance condition is not met | reported. A second repeat is preregistered before any proposal |

**Reported, not criteria:**
- across F2 (J18) and F3, F5 and F6 (J21), Y against W in-batch on four extractions;
- `label-residual-budget` and C1 by majority, for each plan;
- node counts and characters.
