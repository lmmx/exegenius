## Reading (fixed now)

1. **Agreement:** sub-step agreement between each plan's Sonnet judge and its first Opus judge (J11), and Spearman's ρ between their totals.
   - Met if agreement is at least 0.80 and ρ at least 0.90.
2. **Same-model bias:**
   - **The gap:** for each plan, $g$ = Opus judge's total minus Sonnet judge's total.
   - **The bias:** the mean $g$ over Opus-produced plans, minus the mean over Sonnet-produced plans.
   - **Flagged** if the bias is at least 2 sub-steps, that is, if Opus judges credit Opus plans by 2 or more relative to Sonnet plans.

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 met, 2 not flagged | no same-model bias evident on the checklist. Checklist screens may use Sonnet judges, with one Opus judge kept per compared pair as a check |
| 2 flagged | the S2 and S3 model effects are marked "possibly inflated by same-model judging" in the journals and capability map. A Sonnet-judge primary cross-check on J10's six plans is queued |
| 1 not met, 2 not flagged | Sonnet judges are not used for the checklist. Nothing else changes |
