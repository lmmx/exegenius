# S4 (preregistered): Opus at max on F8, against Opus at xhigh

**Status:** written and committed before the run, on the author's go-ahead ("go ahead with the Opus max run on F8"). The pipeline default is unchanged whatever S4 shows. Fable is not launched.

## The decision it informs

**Whether the argument task on this paper leaves room for a stronger model.**
- On F8, Opus `xhigh` (S3F8OX) is short of the ceiling: faithful 16 of 19 (J10) and checklist 19 of 26 (J11).
- On F1, Opus `max` reached 19 of 19 faithful and 24 of 26 on the checklist.

If Opus `max` closes F8's gap, a Fable argument pilot on this paper could at most tie. If it does not, F8 is where the pilot runs, against the better of the two Opus configurations.

## Design

**The run, S4F8OM:**
- a copy of E6's project F8 (background accepted), with the argument task rendered by the E7 binary (91c4fed);
- the task file checked by diff against S3F8OX's, apart from the project path;
- the prompt `exegenius next` prints, word for word, to a fresh `general-purpose` subagent with `model: "opus"`, `effort: "max"`;
- one run.

**Operational failures and truncation:**
- A run killed by the usage limit is an operational failure: its scratch files are moved aside, and it is rerun.
- A `max_tokens` stop inside the run is not. It is a property of the configuration, so the run is judged as it is, and the stop is reported (`max_tokens_stops` in the registry).

**Judging:**
- **J12, primary:** S4F8OM and S3F8OX (re-judged, not reused), blind, three judges each, plus the anchors E1-P4 and E3-M3 with one judge each. J9's instructions and reference pack, unchanged. That makes 8 judgements.
- **J13, secondary:** S4F8OM and S3F8OX on the mechanism checklist (`calibration/rubrics/nlogn-mechanism-checklist.md`), blind, two judges each, with K1's prompt. That makes 4 judgements.

**Amendment, 14:35 UTC, before any S4 result:** J12 and J13 also contain S5F8OX, a repeat of Opus `xhigh` on F8 (`plans/S5.md`), so that run variance is measured in the same batch.
- J12 becomes three plans × 3 judges + 2 anchors = 11 judgements.
- J13 becomes three plans × 2 judges = 6 judgements.
- S4's criteria compare only S4F8OM and S3F8OX, and are unchanged.

## Reading (fixed now)

**Beyond judge noise:** two plans' ranges across their judges do not overlap. That is three judges for the primary faithful count, and two for the checklist total.

**Opus `max` improves on Opus `xhigh` on F8** if:
- its checklist total is above S3F8OX's beyond judge noise;
- with no loss on the primary measures:
  - the gate passes by majority;
  - its central count by majority is at least S3F8OX's;
  - its faithful range does not lie entirely below S3F8OX's;
  - and no judge records a major error.

**Opus `max` reaches the ceiling on F8** if, in addition:
- its faithful median is at least 18;
- and its checklist total from each judge is at least 24, Opus `max`'s level on F1.

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| reaches the ceiling | a Fable argument-task pilot on this paper has no room to show a gain. FB1 moves to a second document, or to auditing the key step; it is not run on F8 |
| improves but does not reach the ceiling | FB1's pilot runs on F8 against Opus `max` |
| does not improve | Opus `max` adds nothing over `xhigh` on F8; FB1's pilot runs on F8 against Opus `xhigh` |
| `max_tokens` stop, any outcome | reported. It counts against adopting `max`, whatever the scores |

Every action is a proposal to the author. Nothing is launched from this table without being reported first.
