# E6 plan (preregistered): confirming the key-step instruction on fresh extractions, with an error criterion

**Status:** written and committed before any E6 run.

## Why

**E4 (paired on E1's extractions, Sonnet at high) found the key-step instruction:**
- raised core derivations in 5 of 5 pairs (median +6);
- raised the central count in 4 of 5 pairs, with one tie (median +3);
- passed the central gate in 3 of 5 plans, against 0 of 5.

**It failed the preregistered rule.** One variant plan made two major errors in the central derivation, and the baseline made none.

**E5 found Opus at medium no better than Sonnet at high** on the central derivation. The model is not the lever for this gap; the task's instructions may be.

E4 left two questions open:
1. **Generalisation:** does the effect hold on extractions other than E1's five?
2. **Errors:** how often do instructed plans carry a major error, against the baseline?

`calibration/PROTOCOL.md` §6 also calls for confirmation on fresh runs before any recommendation.

## Design: fresh extractions, then paired arguments

- **Eight fresh planning projects:** F1–F8, from E1's configuration and paper, byte for byte.
- **Extract and background:** each runs with the baseline binary (master, whose extract and background task files are identical to the variant's, since the variant changes only `ARGUMENT_INSTRUCTIONS`).
- **The split:** each project is copied after its background task is accepted.
  - **FkB:** the argument task rendered by the baseline binary (master af36d6d or later, with no change to `argument.rs`).
  - **FkV:** the same task rendered by the variant (`131026-exp-key-step`, 0c29556).
- **Checked mechanically:** in each pair the task files differ only in the key-step paragraph and the project path.
- **The subagents:** every task goes, with the prompt `exegenius next` prints, word for word, to a fresh `general-purpose` subagent with `model: "sonnet"`, `effort: "high"`. The development session orchestrates and does no task.
- **Restarts:** a subagent killed by an operational failure (a rate limit) before submitting is replaced by a fresh one with the same prompt. Before the retry, the task's scratch directory is emptied, so that no draft is reused (E4's B4 deviation).
- **The evidence:** wiki branch `141026-exp-e6-key-step-confirm`.

## Judging, batch J7

- **Plans:** 18, blind, shuffled with seed 20261021: F1B–F8B, F1V–F8V, and the anchors E1-P4 and E3-M3.
- **Instructions:** J3's, unchanged (the content judge and `calibration/rubrics/nlogn-central-saving.md`).
- **Second judgements:** four, drawn by `random.Random(20261021).sample(range(1, 19), 4)`.

## Measures and decision (fixed now)

**Primary:** the central count (0–9), paired within each project, V against B.

**Error measure:** a plan **errs centrally** if its verdict lists a major error, or a central step judged `wrong`, in a step whose content belongs to C1–C9. The judge's error list names steps; the analysis counts every major error, so this is conservative.

**Supported** if all three hold:
1. **Depth:** V exceeds B on the central count in at least 6 of 8 pairs (exact one-sided sign test: 6 of 8 gives p = 0.145, 7 of 8 gives p = 0.035), with a median paired difference of at least +2.
2. **The gate:** V passes the central gate in at least 4 of 8 plans, and in more plans than B.
3. **Errors:** at most 2 of 8 V plans have any major error, and V's count exceeds B's by at most 1.

A result meeting 1 and 2 but not 3 is reported as "deeper but less reliable". Criterion 3's thresholds are a judgement:
- **Why it is set this way:** one plan in four with an error a reader could be misled by is the most that would justify a change on `master`, since the audit rounds can repair a page but not a plan's structure.
- **What would follow from a 3-only failure:** a follow-up that adds a check for the central derivation, not a rejection of the instruction.

**Also reported:**
- C3–C6 per arm;
- the content judge's counts;
- argument tokens and seconds per arm;
- judge agreement on the second judgements.

**What would follow if supported:** a pipeline-change proposal for the author, with this evidence and E4's. It would put the instruction on `master`, scoped to argument tasks, with a note that the evidence is from one paper. A second document follows before it is called general.

**What would follow if not supported on depth:** the instruction's effect in E4 did not generalise beyond E1's extractions. The next question would be the extraction's role.
