# E8 (preregistered): asking for every variant and every bound's chain

**Status:** written and committed before any E8 run (runbook item 4). The pipeline default is unchanged. The instruction lives only on branch `141026-exp-variants-chains` (4f5ee26), binary `/usr/local/bin/exegenius-chains`.

## Why

**Where the remaining gaps are.** S3 and K1 located what Opus `xhigh` and Sonnet `high` still miss (`docs/journal/2026-10-14-s3-opus-floor.md`, `-k1-checklist.md`):
- **the paper's second construction,** treated in parallel: the complex network's coefficient check, its endpoint corrections and $\eta_{\rm c}$;
- **the chains of estimates behind error, guard and depth bounds:** the depth recurrence $A(e)\le sA(e/m)+E$, its unrolling, and the error chain behind $E_s$.

**The hypothesis.** E7's identity sentence closed the counted-step gap with an instruction, not a stronger model. One more general sentence may close these gaps the same way:

> Where the sources carry out a construction or estimate in more than one variant, develop each variant the target result uses; where a sub-step concludes a bound on an error, a size or a depth, state the recurrence or chain of estimates that produces the bound.

It does not name this paper.

**The competing hypothesis:** these gaps are capability-limited, so Sonnet at `high` will not follow the instruction into the derivations.
