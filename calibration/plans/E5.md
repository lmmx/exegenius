# E5 plan (preregistered): Opus at medium against Sonnet at high, for planning (stage 2)

**Status:** written and committed before any E5 run.

## Why

**S1, the screen,** found Opus reconstructing the counting core of the central saving: all four of C3–C6 in 2 of 4 plans, and the central gate passed in 1 of 2 plans per arm. Sonnet at high reconstructed all four in 0 of 5 plans and passed the gate in 0 of 5 (E3, J3).

**Why Opus at medium:** it is the cheaper Opus arm, and the author's hypothesis. The decision it informs, the default planning model, is consequential, so `calibration/PROTOCOL.md` §6 calls for stage 2 (5 runs) before any recommendation.

The orchestrator does no task and reads no task file.

## Judging, batch J6

- **Plans:** 12, blind, shuffled with seed 20261020: N1–N5, G1–G5, and the anchors E1-P4 and E3-M3.
- **Instructions:** J3's, with the content judge and the central rubric, unchanged.
- **Second judgements:** three, drawn by `random.Random(20261020).sample(range(1, 13), 3)`.

## Measures and decision (fixed now)

**Primary:** the central-derivation count (0–9).

**The comparison:** an exact one-sided Mann–Whitney test, Opus medium over Sonnet high. The Research gate's pass rate per arm is also reported, with exact intervals.

**Opus at medium is preferred for planning on this paper** if both of these hold:
- the median central count is at least +2 over Sonnet high, with p < 0.05;
- Opus medium passes the Research gate in at least 3 of 5 plans.

The Overview gate must hold in at least 4 of 5 Opus plans. A distinguished count difference without 3 of 5 gate passes is reported as "better, not yet Research-grade".

**Also reported:**
- the content judge's counts;
- tokens and seconds per planning task;
- reported cost where available.

Opus is priced higher per token, so token counts are not costs.

**What would follow:**
- **If preferred,** a recommendation to the author that planning on frontier-mathematics papers use Opus at medium, scoped to this paper, with confirmation on a second paper before it becomes a default for the class.
- **If not,** report and stop spending on Opus planning for this paper.
