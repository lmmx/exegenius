# A2: does a source-aware, structured audit catch the errors the current audit cannot?

**Status:** preregistered on 2026-10-17 at 07:14 UTC (ledger). It was drafted as `A2-draft.md` on 2026-10-15 at 11:08 UTC, and the design was completed on 2026-10-17 with no model calls apart from the independent seed review recorded below. **Everything below is fixed before any A2 audit runs.**

**It is kept apart from two other decisions:**
- **The general classifier rule** for future experiments (`A1b-draft.md`). A2's own measurement choice, hand labels, applies to A2 only.
- **The author's decision on source access.** A2 tests it; adopting it remains the author's call.

## The question, and why

The current audit sees the page only, and the support check sees one claim and its quotation. Neither compares a page with the paper. A1 found that no model fixes this: Haiku `high`, Sonnet `high` and Opus `xhigh` all miss the same errors.

A2 asks two questions:
- whether a structured fidelity check helps by itself (arm **I**);
- whether adding access to the paper catches errors that only the source reveals (arm **SI**).

The baseline is the current audit (arm **B**). It decides whether the auditing gap is fixable by a task change, and which one.

## The error set (`docs/journal/2026-10-17-a2-design/`, `tools/a2seed.py`)

- **A1's 24 variants,** unchanged: 6 clean and 18 seeded, with 36 errors and 18 shams.
- **Six new variants,** one per page, each carrying two **source-only** errors. Each new error states a value, hypothesis, scope, conclusion or attribution that the paper contradicts and that the page neither derives nor contradicts, outside any quotation that would reveal it.

- **Counts, with A1's one source-only error (TI-3):** 13 source-only errors, plus 28 page-detectable and 7 quote-only, so 48 planted errors.
- **Checks passed** (`a2seed.py`): each replaced text occurs once on its clean page; the site is visible and unique; no new symbols reach the auditor; bound symbols remain; every quotation is verbatim in the paper; the two errors of a variant are in different sentences; no new error is inside a citation mark.
- **Revisions before any run:**
  - TI-S1 was first written as "the number of wires" and changed, because the page's "W wire streams" would expose it without the paper.
  - The cues were tightened (generic words such as "new" and "half" removed), so that the scorer's "named elsewhere" path cannot credit an unrelated note.
- **Independent review 1** (a fresh Opus subagent that had not seen the author's reasoning; `docs/journal/2026-10-17-a2-design/review-1.json`): 9 of 12 accepted. The changes it led to:
  - **ASF-S1 rejected:** dropping "odd" misstates nothing, since the paper also says "a prime" (1478, 1651). Now an asserted q = 2.
  - **LNR-S1 rejected:** "zero" scratch contents are contradicted by the page's own cyclic row distribution. Replaced by a new seed on the intermediate frames.
  - **RPS-S2 sent back:** the page's later chunk counting exposed it. Replaced by a new seed on what the saving is for.
  - **FAI-S2 and SIC-S1 reworded,** to remove readings that would make them true.
  - **Severities:** three attribution seeds are low (ASF-S2, SIC-S2, TI-S2); the rest are medium.
- **Independent review 2,** of the five changed seeds (`review-2.json`): all accepted. Severities: ASF-S1, LNR-S1 and SIC-S1 high; FAI-S2 and RPS-S2 medium.
- **The key:** `docs/journal/2026-10-17-a2-design/key.json`, with its sha256 in the ledger.

## Arms (`tools/a2setup.py`; the texts are fixed in that file)

All arms run Sonnet `high`, the configuration the audit task runs on in production, as `general-purpose` subagents. Each is given its prompt word for word, up to 20 at a time.

| Arm | Task file | Variants |
|---|---|---|
| **B** | the audit task as rendered by `exegenius` 0.6.0 (sha256 c37a2b3d…), unchanged | the 6 new variants. For A1's 24 variants, B is A1 stage 1's AS answers (same binary and configuration, run 2026-10-15) |
| **I** | B plus the FIDELITY paragraph, inserted before the paragraph on copying quotes: check every definition, hypothesis, step, value and conclusion against what the task file gives, and report what is false, inconsistent, overstated or missing a hypothesis as a `step`, even where the reader could follow it | all 30 |
| **SI** | I plus the SOURCE paragraph: the paper is at `sources/paper.tex`; find the passage behind each claim and compare hypotheses, scope, constants, signs, exponents, inequality directions, strength and credit; give the paper's line. The opening restriction is widened to allow that one file | all 30 |

**What was verified before any run** (2026-10-17):
- The arms differ only as stated: `a2setup.py` checks that each transformed task equals the rendered task plus exactly its arm's insertions. The rendered tasks are identical across arms apart from their root.
- Every copy's `sources/paper.tex` is the key's paper (sha256 6d2b6d00…).
- `exegenius submit` accepts a known-good answer under the I and SI texts. A1's accepted answer for a1-74407e was replayed in throwaway copies.
- The answer schema is unchanged: errors are reported as `step` gaps.

**Copies:** 66, in the scratchpad's `a2/runs/A2{B,I,SI}1/` (B 6, I 30, SI 30). Descriptions are `A2<arm>1 <task id>`.

**Isolation:** `a1collect.py --allow sources/paper.tex` treats a read of that file as allowed. Any other read outside a task's own directory is contamination: the task is excluded and rerun once in a fresh copy.

## Measures, per arm (one run; 80% Wilson intervals)

| Measure | Definition | n |
|---|---|---|
| **R_src** (primary) | source-only errors named | 13 |
| **R_page** (primary) | page-detectable errors named (A1's R_A) | 28 |
| R_quote | quote-only errors named by the audit | 7 |
| **F_A** (primary) | asserting gaps per clean page, outside native defects and adjudicated genuine finds | 6 pages |
| F_A raw | the same, without adjudication | 6 pages |
| **F_sham** (primary) | share of sham sites with an asserting gap | 18 |
| Descriptive | recall per type; gaps per page; submits and rejections; cache read per task | |

**Named:** as in A1, `plant.py score` credits an error named at its sentence, a linked sentence or its paragraph, or by an asserting gap elsewhere that mentions one of its cues. **Whether a gap asserts an error is decided by hand labels, which are primary,** applied with `tools/plant_labels.py`. `ASSERTS` is reported beside them.

**Labelling** follows A1's rule (`docs/journal/2026-10-15-a1-stage1/labels-rule.md`):
- All gaps from all three arms are written by `plant.py score --notes` into one shuffled file, blinded to arm.
- Before labelling, a copy masks paper line numbers ("line 1397", "l. 1397"), and the words "paper" and "source(s)", as "[ref]".
- The labels are committed before agreement is computed or the arm map is read.
- **A known limit:** SI's notes may still reveal their arm by content.
- B's notes on A1's variants were labelled in A1. They are labelled again here, blind. Agreement with the A1 labels is reported as intra-rater reliability.

**Adjudication of clean-page alarms:**
- Every asserting gap on a clean page that is not at a native defect is checked against the paper before the arm map is read.
- If it identifies a genuine misstatement, it counts as a "genuine find", is reported, and is excluded from F_A.
- F_A raw is reported beside it.

## Reading (fixed)

**The audit standard** is A1's: R_page ≥ 0.50, with its 80% lower bound ≥ 0.35; F_A ≤ 0.5 per clean page; F_sham ≤ 0.10. **For SI, it also includes R_src ≥ 0.50.**

**Beyond noise:** the 80% Wilson intervals do not overlap.

Outcomes are read in this order; the first that holds applies:

| # | Outcome | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SI meets the standard, R_src included, and SI's recall on source plus quote errors (20) is beyond noise above B's | **a source-aware, structured audit closes the blind spot at this scale.** Proposed as a task change, with measured cost; the author decides (a CLI change and a default) |
| 2 | SI's source plus quote recall is beyond noise above B's, but SI fails a false-alarm bound | the source helps but costs false alarms: the trade-off is reported; no proposal |
| 3 | I meets the standard, and SI is not beyond noise above I on source plus quote recall | structure, not the source, is what helps on what was tested. The instruction alone is proposed; source-only errors stay uncaught |
| 4 | neither I nor SI meets the standard | auditing stays unresolved at Sonnet `high`. The next step is SI on Opus `xhigh`, preregistered separately |
| 5 | none of the above | inconclusive: reported as such |

**Also reported:** whether SI's R_src is beyond noise above I's, which says whether the source adds to structure.

**Stage 2:** no automatic second run, following the author's direction of 2026-10-15 that a threshold narrowly triggered does not by itself justify one. A second run of I and SI is proposed to the author only if a primary point estimate lies within 0.10 of its threshold **and** the best and worst possible second run would put the pooled result in different outcome rows. Its cost is the first run's measured cost.

## Cost (estimates, not converted)

- **Runs:** B 6 × ~0.15 M and I 30 × ~0.15 M (A1's AS figure). SI is 30 × ~0.3–0.8 M, since it reads parts of a 5,361-line file through tools. Together **~15–30 M**.
- **Orchestration:** ~10–20 M. **Total: ~25–50 M.**
- **Not counted:** the hand labelling (about 300–500 gaps) and the adjudication need no model.

## Weaknesses (stated, not assumed solved)

1. **Labeller blindness is partial.** SI's notes may reveal the arm by content, even masked.
2. **The seeds were written by this session.** An independent reviewer checked them (ledger), but they were chosen by one writer, on six pages of one paper.
3. **Sample size:** 13 source-only errors per run give wide intervals. One run is a screen.
4. **B on A1's variants** reuses answers from 2026-10-15: the same binary and configuration, but not run alongside I and SI.
5. **SI's reading is unconstrained,** so its cost and thoroughness vary by task. That is realistic for production, but noisy.
6. **FIDELITY changes the audit's stated purpose** and may raise false alarms. F_A and F_sham measure this.
7. **The adjudicator** of clean-page alarms is this session, not fully blind to arm.
8. **Production scope:** a production version would need a CLI change (the audit task's text) and a decision on how auditors reach the source.
