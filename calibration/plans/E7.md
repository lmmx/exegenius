# E7 plan (preregistered): asking the key step for its identities and exceptions

**Status:** written and committed before any E7 run.

## Why

**E6 found the key-step instruction:**
- deepens plans in 8 of 8 pairs and reaches the Technical calibre in 7 of 8;
- adds no major errors;
- passes the central gate in only 2 of 8.

In five of the six instructed plans that failed the gate, the shortfall was step C4, rank as dimension change. C4 has two parts:
1. the identity $\operatorname{rank}(P_V-P_U)=\dim V-\dim U$ for nested labels;
2. the $N$ rank-one source edges that are its exceptions.

The judges found each failing plan stating one half, or the conclusion without its mechanism.

**The hypothesis.** The instruction asks for sub-steps down to the sources' reasoning, but not for what makes a counted step hold. A general sentence asking for the identity behind a count, bound or equality, and its exceptions, should close that half-statement. The sentence does not name this paper.

## Design: paired on E6's extractions

- **The variant V′:** exegenius branch `141026-exp-key-step-identity` (91c4fed). It appends one sentence to the key-step paragraph:

  > Where a sub-step concludes from a count, a bound or an equality, state the identity, lemma or definition that makes it hold, and every case the sources treat as an exception to it.

- **The projects:** for each of E6's projects F1–F8 (as copied at the split, background accepted), a third copy, FkW, gets the argument task rendered by V′.
- **Checked mechanically:** the task files of FkV and FkW differ only in the appended sentence and the project path.
- **The subagents:** `general-purpose`, `model: "sonnet"`, `effort: "high"`, with the prompt `next` prints, word for word.
- **Retries:** a retry after an operational failure first moves the task's scratch files aside.
- **The comparison:** W against V, E6's instructed plans, both re-judged in one batch.
- **The evidence:** wiki branch `141026-exp-e7-key-step-identity`.

## Judging, batch J8

- **Plans:** 18, blind, shuffled with seed 20261022: F1V–F8V, F1W–F8W, and the anchors E1-P4 and E3-M3.
- **Instructions:** J3's, unchanged.
- **Second judgements:** four, drawn by `random.Random(20261022).sample(range(1, 19), 4)`.

## Measures and decision (fixed now)

**Primary:** C4 reconstructed, per pair. Pairs where only W reconstructs it are compared with pairs where only V does, by an exact one-sided sign test on the discordant pairs.

**Supported** if all four hold:
1. **C4:** W reconstructs C4 in at least 5 of 8 plans, and the discordant pairs favour W by at least 3, for example 4 to 1 or 3 to 0.
2. **The gate:** W passes the central gate in at least 4 of 8 plans.
3. **Depth is not lost:** the median paired difference in central count, W − V, is at least 0.
4. **Errors:** at most 2 of 8 W plans have a major error, and W's count exceeds V's by at most 1.

**Also reported:**
- C3–C6 per arm;
- the content judge's counts;
- argument tokens and seconds;
- judge agreement.

**What would follow if supported:** a proposal to the author covering both sentences (the key-step paragraph with this addition), with E4, E6 and E7 as evidence, for the argument task on `master`.

**What would follow if not supported:**
- **Recommend the E6 instruction alone,** as a depth improvement.
- **Treat C4 as a check-side problem.** That means a plan audit, a software change for review, not more prompt wording.
