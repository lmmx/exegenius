## Measures

1. **The plan:** the central rubric and content judge on its argument, one blind judgement (as J3–J8).
2. **The pages:** the chain walk (`calibration/fidelity/chain-walk-v1.md`) on the built wiki. To share a batch, W1's wiki is judged blind together with the three earlier full wikis from the chain-walk baseline. Each wiki gets three independent judges, and each step is decided by majority, as E7 proposed for the gate.
3. **The trace:**
   - for each central step the plan reconstructs and the wiki does not, the stage where it was lost: write, rewrite, depth rule, or never written;
   - for each step the wiki has and the plan lacked, where it was added.
4. **Correctness:** the chain walk's `wrong` verdicts, plus a claim-by-claim check of the page or pages carrying C3–C6, as in T1.
5. **The audit rounds** (`exegenius status`): gaps per round, against the last full run.
6. **Cost:** tasks, tokens and seconds per stage, from the transcripts.
