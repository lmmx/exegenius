## Reading (fixed now)

**Stage 1:** a configuration holds on an extraction if its checklist range is not below E9FkY's. It proceeds to stage 2 if it holds on both.

**Stage 2:** a configuration stays at the ceiling if, on both extractions:
- the gate passes by majority;
- C1 is reconstructed by majority;
- its faithful range is not below E9FkY's;
- and it has no more major errors.

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| Haiku `high` stays at the ceiling | Haiku is the planning candidate under the combined instruction; confirm on more extractions |
| Sonnet `medium` stays, Haiku does not | Sonnet `medium` is the candidate |
| neither | Sonnet `high` is the cheapest adequate planner measured |
