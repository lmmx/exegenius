### The run

- **Configuration:** E9F4Y's `exegenius.toml` (title, target, reader, source, url) with the production defaults: `rounds = 3`, `stop_early` default (true), `[checks]` defaults (coverage `reject`, depth `sections`), and `[simulation]` defaults (`rounds = 2`, `budget = 15`). The binary is the installed `exegenius` 0.6.0 at master `abdd104`. The source-aware audit is #14.
- **Models, from `calibration/policy.md`:**
  - extract, background and argument: Sonnet `high`;
  - write: Sonnet `high`, with the writer exception below;
  - audit, support, outline and overloads: Sonnet `high`;
  - goals and grade: Sonnet `high`;
  - read and baseline: Haiku `high`.
- **Writer exception, decided before the plan exists.** Opus `xhigh` writes, and rewrites, at most two pages of the new plan:
  - (a) the page whose "Steps this page derives" carries E9F4Y 0015's step: subspace labels whose dimension changes give total edge rank $s<Wm$ through the loss ratio $L/N=100/539$ or $101/539<1/2$;

  If the content is spread over several pages, the page that derives the defining inequality is chosen ($s<Wm$ for (a), $s/W<m^\tau$ for (b)). One page carrying both counts once. A content no page derives gets no exception. The choice is posted here from the task files before any write task starts. Every other page is written by Sonnet `high`.
- **Subagents:** `exegenius-planner`, `exegenius-task` and `exegenius-reader` cannot be loaded in this session. Copied to `~/.claude/agents/` mid-session, they are still not found.
  - So, as in calibration, each task runs in a fresh `general-purpose` subagent, with the model and effort above and the prompt `exegenius next` prints, word for word.
  - That prompt is preceded by the body of the agent definition `next` names for the task, plus one line restricting tools to that definition's list (Read, Write, Bash).
  - This is a recorded deviation: the restriction is an instruction, not a mechanical limit. Any reader call to another tool is recorded, as in SR1.
- **Bounds:** at most 3 audit rounds and 2 reader rounds, as configured. The run is monitored with `exegenius status`. Project state is pushed to the branch at every stage boundary. The pages are snapshotted when the audit rounds end, before the readers start.

### Primary criteria (overall verdict)

**P1, completion.** `exegenius next` reaches `done`, with every task accepted and the site built, within the bounds above.

**P2, the plan keeps its calibrated standard.** The run's argument is judged with the primary plan judge: J3's instructions and the central-saving table, as in J21. The judge text is in `docs/journal/2026-10-14-e10-combined/judge/judge-prompt.md`. Three blind Opus judges judge the argument, with the reference pack from `tools/prepare.py`; `tools/majority.py` summarises. P2 passes when:
- the central gate passes (C4, C5, C6 and C9 reconstructed by majority, no step `wrong`);
- 9/9 central steps are reconstructed by majority;
- the median faithful count is 19/19;
- the median major-error count is 0.

This is the level the final planning configuration reached on all seven plans judged (E9, E10).

**P3, the built wiki carries the central chain.** `calibration/fidelity/chain-walk-v1.md`, unchanged. Three blind Opus judges read the built markdown wiki, copied to a directory with nothing else, and the paper. P3 passes when C4, C5, C6 and C9 are `reconstructed` by majority and no step is `wrong` by majority.
- The reconstructed count, `reason_given` and the breaks are reported against the baseline (journal 2026-10-13): best older wiki W1 at 6/9 with 5 breaks; W2 at 4/9 with 2 `wrong`; W3 at 2/9.

**P4, no misleading mathematics anywhere.** Every page written by a write task, as it stands in the final wiki, goes to the page judge (J22/J24/J26 protocol, `tools/w3judge.py`'s text). Its assignment comes from its final write task file ("This page in the argument" and "Steps this page derives"). Three blind Opus judges judge each page. P4 passes when no page has a median major-error count above 0.

**P5, the central derivations are derived.** Every page the chain walk cites, by majority, for one of C3–C9 meets the page standard: at least ⌈0.9n⌉ of its n assigned steps faithful, by median, and median major errors 0. C3–C9 are the steps from labels to the exponent; C1 and C2 are set-up.

**Verdict:**
- **PASS:** P1–P5 all hold.
- **FAIL:** any of P2–P5 fails, or P1 fails because of the pipeline (a binary error, a task that cannot be accepted, the bounds exceeded).
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** the run cannot complete for a reason outside the pipeline (quota, container loss), or a criterion has no majority (for example a gate step split three ways).

**What is deliberately not required for PASS, and why.** The full page standard on every page is reported (S1 below), not required.
- Calibration already established that no writer meets it on key-step pages: Sonnet `high` on 4 of 12 key-step page-runs, in first drafts without audits (`capability-map.md`).
- Requiring it would restate that known result, not test whether the stages work together.
- P4 (no major error anywhere) and P5 (the full standard on every page carrying C3–C9) apply it where the paper's central derivation lives.

### Secondary measures (reported, not part of the verdict)

- **S1:** the share of pages meeting the full page standard, against W3's first drafts (Sonnet `high` 4/12 key-step page-runs; Opus `xhigh` 4/6). It shows what the audit rounds add.
- **S2:** audit convergence per round (`exegenius status`), open gaps when the rounds end, and support gaps.
- **S3:** cost in measured cache read per stage (`tools/spend.py`), against the estimate below.

### Simulated readers (a separate verdict, because their effectiveness is unvalidated)

The readers' own coverage scores are not validated measures (SR1). They are reported, and the verdict on the readers rests on the validated page judge.

- **R1, reaching the key step:** coverage on each goal whose key points concern C3–C9, per round, against the no-wiki baseline.
- **R2, locating gaps:** stuck points per page on pages that fail P4 or the page standard, against pages that meet it.
- **R3, repairing gaps:** every page rewritten after a reader round is judged by the page judge before (the snapshot) and after, with three blind judges each.
- **R4, held-out goals:** held-out coverage in round 2 against round 1. It counts as a change only when the two 80% Wilson intervals over key points do not overlap.

**Readers verdict:**
- **HELPED:** either
  - R3: most rewritten pages gain faithful steps, by median, and none gains a major error; or
  - R2: deficient pages carry at least twice the stuck points per page of sound pages, with at least 3 stuck points on deficient pages.
- **HURT:** most rewritten pages lose faithful steps or gain a major error, or R4 falls beyond noise.
- **NOT SHOWN:** otherwise, including when no page is rewritten or too few stuck points fall to tell.

**P4, the error.** Packed selected-bit addition must apply $y_{j_i}\gets y_{j_i}\oplus x_{j_i}$ for $0\le i<f$. The paper sets $j_{f-1}$ aside (line 2054), handles $i<f-1$ with the guarded segments, then "Finally appl[ies] the two-bit XOR to $x_{j_{f-1}},y_{j_{f-1}}$" by the elementary-streams lemma, and notes that for $f=1$ this last step is the whole algorithm (2187–2192). The page reproduces the first sentence ("Omit $j_{f-1}$ temporarily") and ends at "one scan refilling holes in order applies $T_0$", where $T_0$ does the XORs at $j_0,\dots,j_{f-2}$ only. So the page's procedure leaves $y_{j_{f-1}}$ unchanged, and for $f=1$ does nothing. The first draft (0018) already covered only $i<f-1$ with no final step; the 0110 rewrite added the "temporarily" sentence. Three audits (0055, 0148, 0208) did not flag it, and no reader opened the page in either round. Its steps are otherwise faithful (13 of 14 by every judge).
