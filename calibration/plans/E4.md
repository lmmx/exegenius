# E4 plan (preregistered): does asking for the key step make plans reconstruct the central saving?

**Status:** written and committed before any E4 argument ran.

## Why

**The gap at high effort.** In E3, plans at high effort reconstructed the counting core of the central saving (rubric steps C3–C6) in only 1–2 of 5, and none passed the central gate. T1 showed the wiki's writers can carry that core when audits ask for it. So the place to fix is the plan.

**A likely cause, in the argument task's instructions:** *"Deeper steps of a page's own proof can stop at an `infer` step quoting where the sources carry them out"*. Nothing asks for the step the result turns on. The project's own theory names that step the **key step**: the step that does the decisive work (journal 2026-10-11-aha-moments). The paper's own example is the derivation of $s<Wm$.

**The variant** adds one general instruction, written for any document and not naming this paper (exegenius branch `131026-exp-key-step`, 0c29556):

> The key step: the step the target result turns on, the construction or estimate that gives the result its strength (where a saving, a bound or a surprise comes from). A reader who follows only one derivation should follow this one. Develop it with `show` steps down to the reasoning the sources give for it, each sub-step quoting where the sources carry it out, rather than stopping at an `infer` step that quotes its result.

## Design: paired, argument task only

**The pairs:** for each of E1's five high-effort projects (P1–P5, wiki branch `131026-exp-e1-config`), two copies are reset to just before the argument task. The extraction and background are kept, the argument and pending tasks removed.
- **B1–B5:** the argument task as rendered by the baseline binary (master af36d6d: `cc70355` plus a status-message fix).
- **V1–V5:** the same task as rendered by the variant binary.
- The task files differ only in the key-step paragraph, which is verified.

**The agent:** each task goes, with the prompt `exegenius next` prints, word for word, to a fresh `general-purpose` subagent with `model: "sonnet"`, `effort: "high"`. E1 found no difference between definitions at this model and effort.

**Why paired:** pairing on the extraction removes the extraction's variation from the comparison.

## Judging, batch J5

- **Plans:** 12, blind, shuffled with seed 20261019: B1–B5, V1–V5, and the anchors E1-P4 (E1's own argument on P4's extraction) and E3-M3.
- **Instructions:** those of J3, the content judge plus the central rubric (`calibration/rubrics/nlogn-central-saving.md`, unchanged).
- **Second judgements:** three, drawn by `random.Random(20261019).sample(range(1, 13), 3)`.

## Measures and decision (fixed now)

**Primary:** the central-derivation count (0–9) per plan, compared within pairs, V against B.

**Supported** if all three hold:
- V exceeds B in at least 4 of 5 pairs (an exact one-sided sign test at 5 of 5 gives p = 0.031; 4 of 5 gives p = 0.19, so 4 of 5 is reported as suggestive, not distinguished);
- the median paired difference is at least +2 steps;
- V has no more major errors or statements judged wrong than B in total.

**Also reported:**
- gate passes per arm;
- the counting-core steps C3–C6 per arm;
- the content judge's counts, as secondary measures;
- argument tokens and seconds per arm (a fuller derivation may cost more).

**What would follow if supported:** an 8-run confirmation on fresh, full planning runs (extraction included) before anything changes on `master`. The instruction would change the argument task for every document, so it is a pipeline change for review, with the evidence.

**What would follow if not supported:** the plan's gap is not about what the task asks for. The next candidates are the model and effort (S1) and the source's length against the planner's reading.
