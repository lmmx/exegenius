# S1 screening plan (preregistered): other models for planning

**Status:** written and committed before any S1 run existed. **Stage:** screen (`calibration/PROTOCOL.md` §6). Two runs per arm support only a decision to stop spending on an arm, never a claim that an arm is good.

## Question

For planning on the n log n paper, at the configuration E1 and E3 established (`exegenius-planner`, binary `cc70355`, the same configuration and prompts):
- **Economy:** does a cheaper model reach the Overview calibre?
- **The author's hypothesis:** does a stronger model at lower effort match Sonnet at high?
- **The missing counting core:** does a stronger model at high effort reconstruct the core of the central saving (rubric steps C3–C6) that Sonnet at high rarely does?

## Arms (2 runs each)

| Arm | Runs | `model` | `effort` | The decision it informs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opus, medium | O1, O2 | `opus` | `medium` | the author's hypothesis that Opus at medium may equal Sonnet at high |
| Opus, high | Q1, Q2 | `opus` | `high` | whether a stronger model reaches the counting core (Research calibre) |
| Haiku, high | K1, K2 | `haiku` | `high` | whether a cheaper model reaches the Overview calibre |

**Not included:** xhigh and max, which the author asked to hold back; and Sonnet, already measured (E1, E3).

**Recorded effort is checked:** if a model does not support the effort passed, the transcript's recorded effort is reported, and the arm is described by what was recorded.

## Judging, batch J4

**One blind batch:**
- the six S1 plans;
- three anchors from J3, which tie the scale: P4 (the strongest Sonnet-high plan), P5 (a typical one) and M3 (Sonnet medium);
- the instructions of J3, with the content judge and the central rubric;
- seed 20261017;
- second judgements of two plans, drawn by `random.Random(20261017).sample(range(1, 10), 2)`.

## Screening rules (fixed now)

An arm is **screened out** for a calibre when **both** its runs fail by a wide margin:

| Calibre | Screened out if, in both runs |
|---|---|
| Overview | core stated < 9 (half of 17), or a major error |
| Technical | core derived < 5 (half of 10), or a major error |
| Research | central count < 3 (a third of the 9 steps), or a step judged wrong |

**Otherwise** the arm **advances** for that calibre. That says only that it is worth more runs if the decision it informs is wanted.

**What would be reported as a signal**, not a finding:
- an arm whose two plans both pass a calibre's gates;
- an arm that reconstructs C3–C6, which no Sonnet-high plan did together.

## Resources

Tokens and seconds per subagent, as in E3, and the runner's reported session cost. Opus and Haiku token prices differ from Sonnet's, so token counts are not costs. Any cost comparison uses the session's reported figure, with its basis.
