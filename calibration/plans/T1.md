# T1 plan (preregistered): does the depth rule cut the writer's derivation of the central saving?

**Status:** written and committed before any T1 rewrite ran.

## Hypothesis

**Where it comes from:** the chain-walk baseline (journal 2026-10-13-chain-walk-baseline) and the trace of the page `subset-intersection-construction` in the planner-and-handoff run.
- The plan assigned none of the derivation of the central saving to that page.
- Writers' rewrites added it in rounds 1 and 2, answering "explain the step" notes.
- The round-3 audit then marked six of its steps "cite or cut a step inside added reasoning": the telescoping, $Wm-2N$, $L$, $+2L$, and the rank-one source edges.
- Those notes were never applied, because the run ended after round 3. They are the depth rule's notes, built to stop an infinite regress of explanations.

**The hypothesis:** applying them removes the central chain's counting core from the wiki. The local rule then works against the global explanation.

## Test

**Setup:**
- three copies (R1, R2, R3) of the planner-and-handoff project as it ended (wiki branch `full-v0.6-latex-planner-handoff`, 72f1de8), each with `rounds = 4`, so `exegenius next` hands out round 3's rewrites;
- binary `cc70355`, whose `write.rs`, `audit.rs` and `convergence.rs` are unchanged since that run's code (ec620eb).

**In each copy:**
- only the rewrite of `subset-intersection-construction` is given, with the prompt `exegenius next` prints, to a fresh subagent on Haiku at medium effort;
- the original run's writers were on Haiku (they inherited the author's orchestrator), at an effort that is unrecorded;
- then `exegenius build`.

**Deviations from the original run, stated in advance:**
- **The agent:** the development session cannot load `exegenius-task`, so the subagent is `general-purpose`. Its instructions are the task file. E1 found no definition effect in planning at high effort; for writing, that is untested.
- **The effort:** medium is an assumption.

## Measures (fixed now)

**Primary:** the chain-walk verdicts (`calibration/fidelity/chain-walk-v1.md`) on steps C4, C5 and C6 (rank as dimension change, the budget, $L/N<1/2$), and the central gate.
- **The comparison:** the three rebuilt wikis and the original wiki as control, all blinded together and shuffled with seed 20261018.
- **The control re-judged:** it shows how much of any change is judge variation. The original was judged twice in the baseline, with identical step verdicts.

**Secondary,** mechanical, on the rewritten page against the original page:
- whether each of the six marked sentences survives verbatim, survives with a citation in place of its reasoning, or is gone;
- the page's word count.

## What would count

- **The hypothesis is supported** if, in at least 2 of 3 copies, the gate fails or any of C4–C6 drops from reconstructed while the control keeps them.
- **The hypothesis is not supported** if all three keep C4–C6 reconstructed. For example, writers might cite the paper's lines while keeping the statements.
- **Anything else** is reported as is.

**If supported, the next smallest test is not a pipeline change.** It is a replay of the same rewrite with the six notes' action set to "explain the step" (what the rule would say if the plan owned these steps), to see whether the derivation then stays correct and within the page budget. Changing the pipeline remains a separate, reviewed decision.

## The derivation's correctness, checked first

An independent Opus judge checks the original page's derivation of the counting core against `paper.tex`, claim by claim, before any comparison is read. A derivation worth protecting has to be right.
